Where it wins, where it loses

What it can't do

  • It cannot answer questions. It knows no facts, only how old tales tend to go. For answers, use GlowChat.
  • It writes English only, in the voice of books from a century or more ago, and it carries their old-fashioned ideas too.
  • It loses the thread fast. The phrases sound like a fairy tale, but sentences often go wrong, and characters and plot change from line to line.
  • It remembers only the last few hundred words, so a long tale forgets how it began.

Built from scratch

  1. The tokenizer: our own byte-pair encoder, trained on the books. No tokenizer library.
  2. The model: a small transformer that started from random numbers. No pretrained weights, nothing downloaded from another model.
  3. The training: one laptop processor, no graphics card, public-domain books only.
  4. The engine: plain JavaScript written for this page. No AI library, no WebGPU, no WebAssembly.

The books it learned from

All are in the public domain in the USA and come from Project Gutenberg. Books marked “held out” were never shown to the model: they are the test.

    GlowSpark makes things up: that is all it does. Its tales are generated on your device by a small model trained on old books, and can be odd, wrong or dated. Nothing you type or generate here leaves this page unless you choose to send it to GlowChat.
