      Connect Glow Hands

      Glow Hands is a small program on your PC that lets GlowLead open sites, click, type and fill forms in its own browser window. You can watch and take over at any time.

      One click: GlowLead finds Glow Hands on this PC and connects. Nothing to type. If your browser asks to allow local network access, allow it once.

      Use from your phone

      Your phone's GlowLead can drive Glow Hands on this PC over an encrypted direct link. Show the QR code, scan it with your phone camera, done. Your PC must stay on with Glow Hands running.

      On a phone? Open GlowLead on your PC → Connect browser control → Use from your phone, then scan the QR code with this phone's camera.

      New PC? Install in one step

      Press Win + R, paste this, press Enter. It installs Glow Hands, starts it at every login, and connects this browser automatically.

      powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -c "irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juw12/projects/main/apps/glow/hands/install-glow-hands.ps1 | iex"
      Connect manually with a pairing code
