About
GlowRoom is a control room for a crew of AI bots: watch who is working, follow every tool step, and dispatch goals. Goals you dispatch are done for real by Glow AI (free by default; sign in with your Glow account for stronger models, or use your own key in AI brain): Scout reads the live web, Byte runs real code, Pixel draws graphics. The optional Run simulation switch adds pretend background activity.
