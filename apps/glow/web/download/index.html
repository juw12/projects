    Questions

    Is Glow free?

    Yes. Glow, GlowChat, GlowRoom and GlowLead are free with no sign-up. A free Puter sign-in unlocks faster AI models, and the optional Glow Pro plan only raises limits for big missions.

    Is there a Glow app for iPhone and iPad?

    Yes, for free: open Glow in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen like any app, works offline and keeps its own storage. Glow isn't in the App Store, because Apple charges developers 99 US dollars a year to list apps there.

    Does Glow work offline?

    Yes. Every Glow app opens without internet once you have visited it, and your chats stay on your device. Download Glow Offline in GlowChat (Settings → Offline AI on this device) and GlowChat keeps answering with AI that runs on your device's graphics chip. Web research, the AI crew and cloud models need internet.

    Is Glow on Google Play or the Microsoft Store?

    Not yet. Download Glow directly from this page: the Android app is a signed APK and the Windows setup installs every Glow app with a Start menu entry and an uninstaller.

    Where are my chats stored?

    In your own browser on your device. Nothing is stored on a Glow server. You can sync chats through your own Puter account, or export them from Profile & settings.

    Which languages does Glow speak?

    English, Bahasa Indonesia, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese, and the AI answers in your language.
