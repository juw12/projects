OpenRouter: free cloud models

Sign up at openrouter.ai/keys and create a key. Models marked free cost nothing (rate-limited).

Free keys with big daily limits: Google Gemini, Groq and Mistral

No card needed. Gemini: get a key at aistudio.google.com/apikey (Gemini Flash, Flash-Lite and more; your limits are shown in AI Studio). Groq: console.groq.com/keys (very fast GPT-OSS 120B, Llama 3.3 70B, Kimi; about 1,000 requests and 200,000 tokens a day per model). Mistral: console.mistral.ai/api-keys (free Experiment plan, no card: Mistral Medium, Large, Small and Codestral; your limits are shown in the console). Each key has its own limit, so adding all three gives you the most free AI: when your Puter allowance runs out, or one key hits its limit, Glow moves on to the next free key by itself, then Glow Free.
