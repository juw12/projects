🏪 GlowFounder

Test a business idea before you spend money on it. Simulated buyers walk up to your stall, read your pitch and decide. You see who buys, who passes and why.

Loading the studio. The first visit downloads about 14 MB, so it can take a minute.

Honest limits

  • Simulated buyers are not customers. Read the buy rate as an upper bound and a cheap first filter, then run a real launch test.
  • Quick sim is a set of rules with a fresh random draw of buyers, not an AI. Run it a few times: the buy rate moves from run to run.
  • AI buyers run on free AI models. Your pitch and the buyer profiles are sent to those services. They can be slow, busy or simply wrong.
  • Your projects and your last panels are saved in this browser only. Clearing the site data removes them.
  • The numbers are worked out by Python running inside your browser. That is why the first visit downloads about 14 MB.
  • The studio itself is in English for now.

GlowFounder is part of Glow: free, open source, no ads. It is built on Founder Studio. Founder Studio on GitHub
