🤖 GlowBot

Your Glow AI team inside Telegram. Give it a goal: GlowLead plans it, hands the steps to agents, checks the result and reports back. It works in private chats and in groups, and it is free.

Connect your Telegram bot

You do this once. Telegram's own @BotFather makes the bot and gives you its token; you paste the token here.

  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather and send /newbot. Open @BotFather
  2. Give the bot a name, then a username that ends in "bot".
  3. Copy the token @BotFather sends you and paste it below.
  4. Tap Connect. Then open the Telegram link that appears here and press Start. That makes you the owner.

    What you can tell it

    Send a command on its own (like /room) to switch mode. After that, whatever you type goes there. While a mission runs, reply to steer the team.

    /lead
    Give it a goal. GlowLead plans it, hands the steps to agents, checks the result and reports back.
    /room
    Give it a topic. The GlowRoom crew researches it on the live web.
    /ask
    One agent, one job. Name the agent first, like /ask scout, then the task.
    /agents
    Who is on the team.
    /chat
    Plain chat that remembers the last few turns.
    /agent
    GlowHarness: an agent with tools that asks before it saves anything.
    /news
    Today's AI news.
    /spark
    A tiny AI we built from scratch writes a short story from your opening line (in English).
    /apps
    Buttons that open the Glow apps.
    /daily
    AI news every day at a time you set (UTC+7), like /daily 07:30. /daily off stops it.
    /status
    What is running, your mode, language and jobs used today.
    /stop
    Stops the running job.
    /lang
    Changes the language.
    /help
    Everything it can do.
    /access
    Owner only. /access public lets anyone use the bot; /access private closes it again.

    Try: /lead plan a 3-day trip to Bali on a small budget

    Using it in a group

    Add the bot to a group and everyone there shares one team.

    • The owner lets a group in just by adding the bot to it. If someone else adds a private bot to their group, it says so and leaves.
    • In a group it answers commands and replies to its own messages. That is how Telegram's privacy mode works.
    • To make it hear plain @mentions too, turn privacy mode off in @BotFather: Bot Settings, then Group Privacy.
    • Only the person who started a job, or the owner, can press that job's buttons.
    • One job at a time per chat. /stop ends the current one.

      Honest limits

      • It runs on free AI models. They can be slow, busy or simply wrong. Check anything that matters.
      • Research uses Bing and Wikipedia search and reads pages directly. Some sites refuse that, so a step can come back empty.
      • One job at a time per chat. Everyone except the owner gets 20 jobs a day per chat.
      • GlowSpark is a tiny model we trained ourselves. It writes short simple stories and nothing else.
      • What you send goes to the bot server and on to the free AI services it uses. The server keeps a short memory per chat so it can follow up. Do not send secrets.
