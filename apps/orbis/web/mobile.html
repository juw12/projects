How it works

Same order on every platform. Every suggestion keeps its source and reason.

1
Connect
You choose which source to connect. Nothing is read without your consent.
2
Detect
Orbis spots a possible reply or a commitment.
3
Explain
It shows the reason and the original message.
4
Decide
You confirm, edit, snooze or dismiss. Nothing is sent without your approval.
5
Track
Orbis watches for the resolution and keeps the item open until it is done.
6
Review
All open items in one place, and a short morning summary.

Setup

Add it to your home screen

Three simple steps to install Orbis on your phone.

1

Open the app in your phone browser

Navigate to app-mobile.html using Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android).

2

Tap the browser menu

iOS: Tap the Share icon (square with arrow). Android: Tap the three-dot menu.

3

Choose "Add to Home screen"

Select the option and confirm. Orbis will appear on your home screen like a native app.

Note: Exact steps vary by browser and device. Some browsers may not support home screen installation.
