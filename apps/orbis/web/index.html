Product Promise

Orbis helps people keep their commitments and communicate clearly, while their conversations, identity and work stay protected. It is designed to reduce the effort of chasing replies, remembering promises and finding context, and to keep work and personal life in separate spaces. After work, a Growth layer helps people turn learning and personal goals into visible, measurable output.

Foundation

Core Principles

Privacy is the protection layer

Built into every feature. Not a marketing theme.

Human in control

AI suggests with a reason. The person decides. Nothing is sent without approval.

Explainable

Every suggestion shows why it appeared and the source it came from.

Work and personal are separate

Separate spaces, quiet hours and urgent-only exceptions by default.

Self-chosen identity

Aura is created and edited by the person, never inferred from their messages.

Fair in hiring

Personality never ranks, filters or rejects candidates. Human review is required for hiring decisions.

Accessible

Screen-reader support, plain language, text labels alongside color.

Start from real use

Build from a real problem, test with real people, and show what failed as well as what worked.

Evidence before expansion

Each layer earns the next one through measured results.

Rules

Explainability — every suggestion shows its reason and source.

Uncertainty — inferred items labeled as suggestions, never facts.

No invention — no invented date, owner or amount.

Correction — dismissed or wrong items teach the system and are easy to fix.

Sending — nothing is sent without explicit approval.

Aura

Rules

Created by the person — self-reflection quiz or by hand; never inferred from messages.

Visibility — private by default; shared only per item.

Used for — reflection, communication preferences, and context in an introduction the person accepts.

NEVER used for — ranking, filtering, rejecting or scoring any person, or for employer decisions.

Sensitive content — health, religion, political views not collected; written content of this kind is warned about and excluded from matching and search.

Control — edit, hide or delete at any time.

Organization

Culture

Culture is an optional set of working norms an organization can publish: hours, tone of messages, approval steps and quiet-hour defaults.

Rules

Optional — never forced beyond the workspace.

Visible — employees can read every rule that applies to them and why.

Scope — workspace only; personal life is outside its reach.

Admin limits — admins set rules but do not see private message content.

Personal Development

Growth Layer

A personal learning and progress layer for after-work time, built on the 5–9 Freedom Lab method. Separate from work data.

5-Stage Method

1
Understand
Real problem, baseline, interviews
2
Organize
Simplest repeatable system
3
Explain
Honest evidence and lessons
4
Create
Turn proven workflow into output
5
Empower
Others adapt it and get results

Features

  • Curriculum
  • Key results (measurable goal with baseline, target and current value)
  • Session log
  • Weekly review (output, blockers and energy reviewed as evidence, not guilt — no streaks that punish missed days)
  • Before and after metrics
  • Progress card (opt-in summary the person designs)
  • Export (everything can be exported or deleted)

Growth Rules

  • Separate from work
  • No work-message analysis unless explicitly opted in
  • Private by default
  • No ranking/leaderboards
  • No guaranteed outcomes
  • Rest counts
[VALIDATION REQUIRED]

Growth is a personal tool. It does not coach, diagnose or decide a person's purpose, and it does not claim that tracking will create wealth, discipline or success.

Protection

Privacy Protection Layer

Minimum data

Only what each feature needs.

Consent

Clear, revocable, per-source.

Visibility

You see what is stored and why.

Control

Edit, export or delete at any time.

Separation

Work and personal data never mix.

Kill switch

Disconnect any source instantly.

Audit

Logs of access and changes.

Aura and portfolio fields do not ask for health, religion, political views or similar sensitive data. If a person writes such content, Orbis warns them, and the content is excluded from matching, search and summaries.

[VALIDATION REQUIRED]

Legal baseline. One design, built to the strictest common standard: data minimization, consent, deletion rights, explainability and human review. Each market needs a short addendum confirmed by counsel: Indonesia (PDP Law), the EU (GDPR and AI Act) and US states (automated hiring rules).

  • Platform access — Launch with sources that allow it
  • Noisy suggestions — Easy dismiss; learn from corrections
  • Fake profiles — Verification stages and report queue
