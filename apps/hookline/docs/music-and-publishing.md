# Real songs, remixing and publishing — Hookline 1.2

## Correcting the earlier explanation

The earlier blanket statement that copyrighted songs cannot be remixed was too
broad. Hookline can process user-provided audio, including commercial recordings;
there is no song-title or copyright-status gate in the DSP pipeline. An assistant
can also help transform audio the user provides. These technical capabilities do
not establish whether a particular use is legally permitted.

Editing a local file and publishing/distributing a derivative recording are
separate questions. Personal or private use is not automatically a legal
exception everywhere. Buying a file or subscribing to a streaming service does
not by itself grant remix/publication rights. Exceptions depend on jurisdiction
and facts; there is no universal safe clip length or pitch/speed change.

## Why CapCut and similar apps can offer music

They provide certain music under particular agreements, and they also let users
supply their own content. Technical upload/edit access is not a blanket license.
Track, account, territory, platform, advertising and modification restrictions
can differ. A creator may also have independent permission; another creator's
video being online is not proof that the use is cleared.

The official **CapCut Materials License Agreement** distinguishes Sounds from
Commercial Sounds. Its Commercial Sounds section currently permits sharing the
video within **CapCut, TikTok and TikTok for Business**, and requires separate
rights for uses outside those permitted platforms. That is not blanket permission
to extract a library recording, alter it elsewhere, export standalone audio or
redistribute a remix across platforms.

For **YouTube Shorts**, eligible music is licensed through specified creation
workflows. YouTube explicitly warns that copyright-protected content used outside
its creation tools may receive a Content ID claim or a removal request. Its
Audio Library is a separate option; read track-specific license and attribution
requirements. “Royalty-free” is not the same as unlimited remix redistribution.

## Practical options

1. **Bring your audio to Hookline.** Preview, adjust pitch/tempo, sequence and
   export locally. Before public distribution, check the rights for that use.
   An independent Paramore mashup normally needs consideration of both the master
   recordings and underlying compositions, plus derivative-use, distribution and
   possibly synchronization rights. An ordinary cover license is not automatically
   permission to reuse the original recording or make a mashup.
2. **Use a destination platform's permitted library workflow.** Prepare your video,
   then add an eligible sound within that platform. Do not assume its license
   transfers into Hookline or onto another social network, or permits standalone
   remix distribution. Check business/commercial restrictions.
3. **Use original or explicitly remix-licensed material.** Verify both the
   recording and composition, permitted modifications, publication channels,
   monetization, required credit, territory and expiry. Check redistribution and
   sublicensing restrictions, even for stock or “royalty-free” tracks.
4. **For a public app catalogue:** negotiate or contract for rights that expressly
   cover end-user remixing and the proposed output distribution. An API key,
   consumer subscription or media download alone is not that agreement. Licensing
   and content-handling arrangements require a separate business/legal workstream.

## What the app actually does

- Local-file editing remains available regardless of undocumented source notes.
- Optional declarations record permission basis, a reference, credit and
  restrictions, along with intended platform and commercial-use preferences.
- Publishing notes always say **unverified**. They do not grant, check or certify
  rights and never produce a “copyright-safe” badge.
- Reports describe the **current active session sources**, not necessarily a
  previous rendered file. “Save current session recipe” preserves current notes;
  “Save rendered recipe” preserves the earlier render's snapshot.
- Portable recipes can include source names and private license notes: review
  them before sharing. No audio, rights certificate or actual license is supplied.
- No commercial-song downloads, streaming-service ripping, DRM bypass or Content
  ID evasion were added. Copyright, rights clearance and virality are not inferred
  from the audio or an editing-checklist score.
