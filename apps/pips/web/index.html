Your desktop pet

  • 23 animals in 3 groups — Land (cat, dog, bunny, bear, panda, fox, hamster, piggy, sprout) walk; Water (fish, whale, octopus, axolotl, penguin, turtle, frog, jellyfish) swim and blow bubbles; Sky (bird, owl, bat, bee, butterfly, dragon) hover and flap their wings.
  • Drag it anywhere — drop it on the taskbar and it wanders along it on its own.
  • Click to tickle (and open Pips), rub to pet, shake it and it gets dizzy.
  • Real moods — happy, sad, tired, hungry, grumpy … it naps when you're away and says “welcome back!”.
  • Cute chat bubbles for break, water and eye-rest reminders, finished pomodoros and daily goals.
  • Make it yours — any colour, cheek colour, size, name; English, Bahasa Indonesia, 繁體中文, 简体中文.

The widget

  • Pomodoro timer — work to short break to long break, auto-starting the next session.
  • Soundscapes — rain, thunder, brown noise, cafe and more, generated locally.
  • Live app tracking — minutes per app, sampled from the foreground window.
  • Daily score — a 1–5 star rating, with goals, streaks, XP, a dashboard and history.

Install

  1. Download Pips-Windows.zip and unzip it anywhere (e.g. Documents\Pips).
  2. Double-click Start Pips.bat. The first start installs two small helpers (pywin32, Pillow).
  3. Choose your language, then click your new pet to open Pips. Right-click it for everything else.
  4. Optional: run install_autostart.ps1 to start Pips with Windows.

Everything stays on your PC — no account, nothing uploaded.
