User guide (PowerPoint)

New in 2.20.0 (2026-10-29)

  • When a friend's pet comes over it now wears a name tag: your friend's name (or the nickname you gave them), ♂/♀ and an online dot. It walks up to your pet, says hi, and looks at your mouse when you point at it.
  • Point at the visitor (or click its name tag) to open its card: whose pet it is, level, how close you two are, and buttons to wave, pet it, give it a snack, visit back or chat. It reacts to everything - hearts, crumbs, happy faces - and your friend sees it too.
  • Rub your mouse over the visitor to pet it. Playing with it makes it stay longer, and your pet comes down from a window or the sky to meet it.

Already have Pips? Keep your current installation and profile: right-click your pet → Help → Check for updates. No reinstall needed.

Your desktop pet

  • 23 animals in 3 groups — Land (cat, dog, bunny, bear, panda, fox, hamster, piggy, sprout) walk; Water (fish, whale, octopus, axolotl, penguin, turtle, frog, jellyfish) swim and blow bubbles; Sky (bird, owl, bat, bee, butterfly, dragon) hover and flap their wings.
  • Drag it anywhere — drop it on the taskbar and it wanders along it on its own.
  • Click to tickle (and open Pips), rub to pet, shake it and it gets dizzy.
  • Real moods — happy, sad, tired, hungry, grumpy … it naps when you're away and says “welcome back!”.
  • Cute chat bubbles for break, water and eye-rest reminders, finished pomodoros and daily goals.
  • Make it yours — any colour, cheek colour, size, name; English, Bahasa Indonesia, 繁體中文, 简体中文.

The widget

  • Pomodoro timer — work to short break to long break, auto-starting the next session.
  • Soundscapes — rain, thunder, brown noise, cafe and more, generated locally.
  • Live app tracking — minutes per app, sampled from the foreground window.
  • Daily score — a 1–5 star rating, with goals, streaks, XP, a dashboard and history.

Install

  1. Click Download Pips for Windows above.
  2. Open Pips-Setup.exe from your browser and click Install Pips.
  3. Choose your language and meet your pet. Pips is also in your Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts.

No Python setup, no ZIP extraction, no commands. Installs only for your Windows account. You can opt in to start with Windows from Pips → Autostart.

This installer is currently unsigned. Windows SmartScreen may show “Windows protected your PC”. If you trust this official download, choose More info → Run anyway if available. On a managed work PC, ask IT if installation is blocked; do not disable security software.

Troubleshooting, existing users & advanced downloads

Already use a ZIP copy? Update inside that copy via Help → Check for updates to keep the same profile. The installer preserves data in its own installation folder; it does not automatically import another ZIP folder.

To remove the installed app, use Windows Settings → Apps → Pips → Uninstall. Your local settings and progress are left in place for a later reinstall.
