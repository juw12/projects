Phone Trail

Your phone screen time as a route

Automaticno typing

Android: get the app. It reads your screen time by itself and keeps a trail for every day.

Download Android app
  1. Open the download and tap Install (allow installs from your browser once).
  2. In Phone Trail tap Open settings and switch on Usage access.
  3. Done: your trail fills in by itself. Tap an app to change its type.

iPhone: Apple keeps Screen Time data for App Store apps only. Free way: take a screenshot of Settings > Screen Time > See All App & Website Activity and tap From screenshot below.

  • Direction = time of day: the trail turns clockwise from morning to night.
