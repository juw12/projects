PIPS - desktop pet + Pomodoro focus widget (Windows 10/11)
=============================================================
Owner: Juwen Lie

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (e.g. Documents\Pips).
2. Double-click  "Start Pips.bat".
   - Needs Python 3 (free from python.org). The first start installs two small helpers.
3. Pick your language (English / Bahasa Indonesia / 繁體中文 / 简体中文).
4. Your pet appears on the taskbar and walks you through a 1-minute interactive guide.

Tips
- Click the pet to open the Pomodoro card. Right-click it for everything else.
- Right-click the pet -> Animal: 23 animals (Land / Water / Sky).
- Drag it anywhere; drop it on the taskbar and it walks there.
- Rub the mouse back and forth over it to pet it.
- Replay the guide: right-click the pet -> Help -> Interactive guide.
- Full user guide: Pips-Guide.pptx (in this folder).
- Start Pips with Windows: right-click the Pips card -> Autostart.

Updates
- Pips checks for new versions by itself and your pet tells you. Click "Update now":
  it downloads, installs and restarts - your settings and history are kept
  (the old version is saved in the backup\ folder).
- Manual check: right-click the pet -> Help -> Check for updates.

Pips stores everything locally in this folder (data\, config.json). Nothing is uploaded.
More: https://juw12.github.io/projects/pips/
