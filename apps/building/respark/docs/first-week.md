# First-Week Action Plan

Day-by-day. 2–3 hours per day. By next Monday you'll have sent 50+ cold emails and booked at least 1 sales call.

---

## Before you start (30 min)

- [x] Brand set to **Respark**. (Done — replaced across `web/`.)
- [ ] Create a free Google account: `respark.reactivation@gmail.com` (or your own + an alias). This is what you send cold emails from.
- [ ] Open `prospects.csv` in Excel/Google Sheets.

---

## Day 1 (Mon) — Find 25 prospects

**Time: 2 hours**

1. Pick your city (the bigger the metro, the more prospects).
2. Go to google.com/maps. Search "plumber in [city]" — open each result, copy: business name, owner name (if visible), phone, website URL, review count.
3. Paste each into `prospects.csv`.
4. Do the same for: dentists, salons, trainers, mechanics. Mix of industries.
5. Stop when you hit 25. Save.

**Done = 25 rows in prospects.csv with full contact info.**

---

## Day 2 (Tue) — Find 25 more prospects + send first 10

**Time: 3 hours**

1. **Hour 1:** Same as Day 1, different industries or neighborhoods. Push to 50 prospects.
2. **Hour 2:** Open `sales-playbook.md`. Pick Template A or C (whichever fits you).
3. **Hour 2:** Personalize Template A for your first 10 prospects. Send from your new email. Subject: "Quick question, [business name]?"
4. Log each send in `prospects.csv` — update status to `emailed`.

**Done = 50 prospects total, 10 cold emails sent.**

---

## Day 3 (Wed) — Send 20 more + follow-up

**Time: 2 hours**

1. Send 20 more cold emails (mix of industries).
2. Check replies from Day 2 sends. Reply to anyone who responded — even if it's "not interested." Thank them politely. (You never know who refers you later.)
3. If anyone wants a call: book it. Use Calendly free tier or just propose a specific time.

**Done = 30 total emails sent, 0+ replies handled.**

---

## Day 4 (Thu) — Send remaining + book calls

**Time: 2 hours**

1. Send the last 20 cold emails. (You're now at 50 sent total.)
2. Reply to any new responses. Book calls where appropriate.
3. If you have a call booked, prep using the call script in `sales-playbook.md`. Practice the opening out loud once.

**Done = 50 emails sent total, calls booked.**

---

## Day 5 (Fri) — Run your calls + refine

**Time: 2–3 hours**

1. Take any calls you have booked. Use the script. Demo `docs/demo.html` on screen-share if possible.
2. After each call: log outcome in `prospects.csv`. Update notes column with what they said.
3. Update `sales-playbook.md` with anything new you learned (objections you heard, phrases that worked).

**Done = calls completed, notes logged, playbook improved.**

---

## Day 6 (Sat) — Send round 2 + try DMs

**Time: 2 hours**

1. Anyone who didn't reply to your first email? Send a short follow-up:
   > "Hi [name] — bumping this up. Quick 15-min call to see if reactivation makes sense for [business name]?"
2. Now try the Instagram/Facebook DM templates from the playbook. Send 10 DMs to local businesses you follow or have interacted with.

**Done = follow-ups sent, DMs started.**

---

## Day 7 (Sun) — Rest + plan next week

**Time: 1 hour**

1. Review `prospects.csv`. Count: emails sent, replies, calls booked, calls done, deals closed.
2. Identify the 3 prospects most likely to close. Send each a personal message.
3. Set next week's target: X more emails, X more calls.
4. If you closed even 1 deal, celebrate. If not, that's normal — most weeks will be 0 deals until week 3 or 4. Keep going.

---

## What "success" looks like at the end of week 1

| Result | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| 0 replies | Normal. Email subject lines need work. Adjust and try again next week. |
| 1–5 replies | You're doing fine. Most replies won't convert yet — that's expected. |
| 1–2 calls booked | You're ahead of 90% of people who try this. |
| 1 closed deal | You've made $497. Keep the momentum. |

The compound effect: most of your closed deals in week 3+ will come from people you emailed in week 1. Don't give up after one quiet week.
