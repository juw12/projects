# Section 01: Listing Titles That Rank (15 prompts)

Etsy titles max out at 140 characters. Etsy’s search engine (called “Etsy Search” or “Marmalead-style” ranking) weights the first few words the heaviest, then scans for keywords across the rest. These prompts force ChatGPT to write titles that earn clicks AND rank.

---

### P01-001 — Long-Tail Keyword Title Builder
**Use when:** You have a product and need a title that hits the exact phrase buyers search.
```
You are an Etsy SEO specialist. Write 5 listing titles for my product.

Product: [PASTE your product description or photo description]
Target buyer: [e.g., new mom, remote worker, dog owner, teacher]
Top 3 keywords I want to rank for: [keyword 1, keyword 2, keyword 3]

Constraints:
- 140 characters max each (count and report)
- Front-load the strongest keyword in the first 40 characters
- Use natural phrasing a human would type, not keyword stuffing
- Include one long-tail phrase (3+ words) per title
- End with a benefit or occasion word (gift, wedding, personalized, custom)

Return titles as a numbered list with character count for each.
```

---

### P01-002 — Title Rewrite From a Competitor’s Top-Seller
**Use when:** You see a competitor ranking well and want to write a similar-but-better title.
```
Here’s the title of a top-ranking Etsy listing:
"[PASTE competitor title here]"

My product is similar but differs in: [e.g., material, color, size, personalization, price point].

Write 5 alternative titles that:
- Target the same primary keyword but use different secondary keywords
- Highlight my differentiator in the first 60 characters
- Avoid copying the exact phrase structure (no plagiarism risk)
- Stay under 140 characters

For each, briefly note which competitor title it’s differentiated from.
```

### P01-003 — Seasonal / Occasion Title Generator
**Use when:** Q4, Valentine’s, Mother’s Day, weddings, Christmas, back-to-school, etc.
```
My Etsy product: [PASTE product or short description]
Upcoming season / occasion: [e.g., Christmas 2026, Valentine's Day, Teacher Appreciation Week]

Write 5 titles that:
- Include the occasion keyword (e.g., "gift for teacher", "stocking stuffer")
- Still rank for evergreen search terms (so the listing sells year-round too)
- Use seasonal phrasing buyers actually search: "Christmas gift", "holiday hostess", "Secret Santa"
- Under 140 characters

After the titles, list 3 tags I should pair with each title to capture both seasonal and evergreen traffic.
```

---

### P01-004 — Title for a Product With Multiple Use Cases
**Use when:** Your product works for several audiences (gift + self-use + occasion).
```
Product: [PASTE description]
Possible use cases: [e.g., wedding favor, baby shower decor, housewarming gift, desk accessory]

Write 3 different 140-character titles, each one anchoring a DIFFERENT primary use case in the first 40 characters. For example, Title A leads with "wedding favor", Title B leads with "baby shower", Title C leads with "housewarming".

This lets me A/B test which use case converts best in my niche.
```

---

### P01-005 — Title That Beats Etsy's "Stuffing" Penalty
**Use when:** You’re tempted to repeat keywords and need a clean, natural title.
```
Draft title I have: "[PASTE your current title]"

Etsy’s algorithm de-duplicates and penalizes keyword stuffing. Rewrite it so:
- Each major word appears only once unless grammatically required
- The same idea isn't repeated in different words (no "handmade - hand crafted - artisan")
- The flow still sounds like a real human describing the item
- Strongest keyword stays in the first 40 characters
- 140 characters max

Return 3 versions: minimal, balanced, and keyword-rich (in case my niche rewards density).
```

---

### P01-006 — Personalized / Custom Product Title
**Use when:** You sell made-to-order items and the title needs to convey customization.
```
Product: [PASTE]
What buyers can customize: [e.g., name, date, color, monogram, photo]

Write 5 titles that:
- Include "personalized", "custom", or "monogrammed" in the first 60 characters (these are high-intent keywords)
- Describe the product clearly without the customization (so the photo/description sells the customization)
- Under 140 characters
- Include a gift-occasion angle: wedding, baby, birthday, anniversary, etc.

After the titles, suggest 3 supporting tags for each.
```

---

### P01-007 — Title for a Low-Competition Long-Tail Niche
**Use when:** You sell something unusual and want to own a tiny niche instead of fighting giants.
```
My product: [PASTE]
My niche angle: [e.g., "for left-handed people", "for IT managers", "for sourdough bakers", "for book club hosts"]

Write 5 titles that:
- Lead with the niche angle (so I rank #1 for that specific search)
- Still include 1-2 broader keywords so I capture adjacent traffic
- Sound specific enough that the right buyer self-identifies
- 140 characters max

For each, estimate the monthly Etsy search volume for the lead keyword using your best guess (Low / Medium / High).
```

---

### P01-008 — Title A/B Test Variants
**Use when:** You have one title and want to test variations scientifically.
```
Current title: "[PASTE]"

Generate 6 alternative titles for A/B testing:
- 2 lead with the product TYPE (e.g., "Wooden cutting board...")
- 2 lead with the BUYER (e.g., "Gift for chef...")
- 2 lead with an OCCASION (e.g., "Housewarming gift...")

All under 140 characters. For each variant, state the hypothesis: which Etsy search intent does it target, and why a buyer would click it over the original.
```

### P01-009 — Title When You Have Zero Sales / No Traffic
**Use when:** Your listing is buried and you suspect the title is the problem.
```
Listing details:
- Product: [PASTE]
- Current title: [PASTE]
- Current tags: [PASTE 13 tags]
- Traffic: [e.g., 0 visits/week, 50 visits/week]

Diagnose what’s likely wrong with my current title in 3 bullets. Then write 5 replacement titles that:
- Fix each diagnosis you identified
- Are designed for Etsy's 2026 search algorithm (relevance + click-through + conversion history)
- Under 140 characters each

End with one bold recommendation: which title to test first and why.
```

### P01-010 — Title for a Product Where You Don’t Know the Buyer
**Use when:** You made something cool but aren’t sure who’d search for it.
```
Product: [PASTE description + photo details]
Material / style: [e.g., minimalist, boho, modern, rustic]
Price point: $[X]

I’m not sure who the buyer is. Give me 5 different titles, each one targeting a DISTINCT buyer persona:
- Title A: gift buyer
- Title B: self-purchase buyer
- Title C: occasion buyer (specify the best occasion)
- Title D: niche hobbyist buyer
- Title E: corporate / bulk buyer

For each, name the persona in one sentence.
```

### P01-011 — Title That Translates a Physical Photo to Search Language
**Use when:** Your product is visual and hard to describe in keywords.
```
Describe my product photo:
[Describe what you see: e.g., "A small ceramic planter, sage green with a matte finish, sitting on a wooden shelf next to a trailing pothos plant. The planter is about 4 inches tall."]

I need Etsy buyers who would type text — not people browsing photos — to find this. Translate the visual into 5 search-friendly titles:
- 140 characters max
- Front-load the object type ("ceramic planter", "small plant pot")
- Include style descriptors ("minimalist", "modern", "boho") only if they match real search behavior
- Avoid flowery language that no one searches

Note: Buyers typing "minimalist sage ceramic planter" should land on this listing.
```

### P01-012 — Bulk Title Generator (Whole Shop Refresh)
**Use when:** You have 20+ listings and want to refresh all titles fast.
```
Here are my current Etsy listings, one per line:
[PASTE list of product names + 1-line descriptions]

For each, write a new 140-character title optimized for:
- Front-loaded primary keyword
- Natural phrasing (no stuffing)
- A clear hook in the last 30 characters

Return as a table: | # | Old title | New title | Why this title ranks |

Cap at 30 listings per request (call me back for the next batch).
```

### P01-013 — Title That Pairs With Etsy Ads
**Use when:** You’re paying for Etsy Ads and need a title that converts cold traffic.
```
Product: [PASTE]
My Etsy Ads daily budget: $[X]
Average CPC in my niche: $[Y]

Etsy Ads traffic is cold — the buyer hasn’t seen my shop before, they typed a search term and clicked an ad. Write 3 titles that:
- Match the EXACT phrasing Etsy Ads buyers use (more literal, less creative)
- Front-load the keyword I’m bidding on: [keyword]
- Include a trust / quality signal in the last 30 characters (e.g., "handmade", "personalized", "premium")
- 140 characters max

For each, predict click-through rate vs. my current title (better / same / worse) and why.
```

---

### P01-014 — Title Optimized for Etsy’s "Related Searches"
**Use when:** You want to capture traffic from Etsy's "Related searches" footer.
```
Primary keyword I want to rank for: [keyword]
My current title: [PASTE]

Etsy shows "Related searches" at the bottom of search results. Buyers click these. Rewrite my title so it:
- Includes 2-3 phrases that Etsy would auto-suggest as related searches (ask me to suggest 10 related search phrases first if unsure)
- Stays natural — title must still read like a human description
- 140 characters max

First, list 10 likely "Related searches" phrases for my primary keyword. Then write 3 titles incorporating the best ones.
```

### P01-015 — Title for Repositioning a Product to a New Niche
**Use when:** A product isn’t selling to your current audience; you want to reframe it.
```
Product that isn’t selling: [PASTE]
Current audience I tried: [e.g., women 25-40, home decor buyers]
New audience I want to try: [e.g., corporate gifting, teachers, dog owners, college students]

Write 5 titles that reposition this product to the new audience:
- New audience’s language and search behavior (not just renaming the product)
- Keep all physical details accurate (don’t lie about what the product is)
- 140 characters max

For each title, write 1 sentence on why the new audience would click instead of scroll past.
```
