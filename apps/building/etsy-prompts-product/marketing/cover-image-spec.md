# Cover Image Design Spec

For the buyer to design (or have designed) a thumbnail that converts at the Etsy/Gumroad search-page size.

---

## Gumroad Cover (recommended: 1280 × 720 px, ratio ~16:9)

**Layout (left to right):**

| Position | Content |
|---|---|
| Left ⅓ (solid color block) | Headline: "150 ChatGPT Prompts" |
| Right ⅔ (product preview) | Mock stack of 6-9 prompt cards fanned out, with one card text-readable |

**Background:** Off-white (#F8F4EE — warm paper) with a soft terracotta accent (#C76E4A) in the corner of the left block.

**Headline text:**
- Line 1 (small caps): "FOR ETSY SELLERS WHO WANT TO SHIP LISTINGS, NOT STARE AT THEM."
- Line 2 (large, serif): "150 ChatGPT Prompts."
- Line 3 (large, serif): "The System."
- Line 4 (smaller): "Titles. Tags. Descriptions. Diagnosis. Updated for Etsy's 2026 algorithm."

**Prompt card preview (the "stack"):**
- Show 6 cards fanned out, each labeled with a section number from the product:
  - "01 / Listing Titles That Rank"
  - "02 / Descriptions That Convert"
  - "03 / Tags & Etsy SEO"
  - "04 / Customer Service Replies"
  - "07 / Mockups & Photography"
  - "09 / Troubleshooting & Diagnosis"
- Each card shows a snippet of an actual prompt (truncated, 2 lines visible)

**Bottom right corner:** Small badge — "PDF + Notion Template"

**Typography:**
- Headline: A clean serif (e.g., Playfair Display, Lora, Source Serif Pro). Available free on Google Fonts.
- Card text: A clean sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Work Sans). Available free on Google Fonts.
- No cursive / handwritten fonts. Looks amateur.

**What NOT to do:**
- No stock photos of women holding laptops
- No AI-generated "person at desk" illustrations (overdone, signals slop)
- No emoji in the headline
- No gradient backgrounds (looks 2022)
- No "ChatGPT" logo (trademark issue, OpenAI may flag)
- No fake review counts (e.g., "★★★★★ 2,500 reviews")
- No "AI" buzzword soup

---

## Etsy Thumbnail (Square 1:1, 2000 × 2000 px minimum)

**Etsy search results show thumbnails at 200-300px.** Design must read at that size.

**Layout:**
- Top ⅓: Solid color block (terracotta #C76E4A) with large white text: "150 ChatGPT PROMPTS for Etsy Sellers"
- Middle ⅓: Product preview (the same 6-card fan, but smaller)
- Bottom ⅓: Solid color block with bold white text: "WRITING SYSTEM • PDF + NOTION"

**Typography at thumbnail size:**
- Top text must be readable when shrunk to 200px wide (so use 80-100pt at full size)
- Bottom text minimum 50pt at full size

---

## Pinterest Pin (Vertical 2:3, 1000 × 1500 px)

**Layout:**
- Top ⅓: Soft beige background with serif text: "Writing your Etsy listings takes 45 minutes?"
- Middle ⅓: Same prompt card fan as Gumroad cover (readable, high contrast)
- Bottom ⅓: Solid terracotta block with white text: "150 ChatGPT Prompts. The System. → [link]"

**Pinterest design rules:**
- Vertical orientation
- High contrast text against background
- Minimal text (Pinterest readers skim)
- Title overlays must be readable when scrolled past quickly

## File Deliverables for the Designer

The buyer should give their designer (or do it themselves in Canva — free) the following files:

1. `/cover-gumroad.png` — 1280×720 px, the cover
2. `/thumbnail-etsy.png` — 2000×2000 px, square
3. `/pin-launch-1.png` — 1000×1500 px, vertical Pinterest pin
4. `/pin-launch-2.png` — 1000×1500 px, alternative pin (different angle / text overlay)
5. `/thumbnail-youtube.png` — 1280×720 px, optional YouTube thumbnail if they make a launch video

**Canva instructions for the buyer:**
- New design → custom size → enter dimensions
- Use the color codes above (paste hex into Canva's color picker)
- Upload the prompt card mockup as an image, OR recreate it in Canva
- Export as PNG (not JPG — sharper text)
- For Gumroad, also export a square crop as fallback

**Free Canva templates to start from:**
- Search "ebook cover" templates in Canva
- Search "ebook" + "modern serif" in Canva
- Customize with the layout above

## Color Palette Reference

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm paper | #F8F4EE | Background |
| Terracotta | #C76E4A | Accent / headlines |
| Deep ink | #1F1A17 | Body text |
| Soft sage | #8FA68E | Secondary accent (optional) |
| White | #FFFFFF | Card backgrounds, text on dark |

This palette is intentionally warm and editorial — not the typical "tech / AI" blue gradient. It signals "real product" and stands out in search results.
