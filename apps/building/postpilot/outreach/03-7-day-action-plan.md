# File 03 — 7-Day Action Plan

Each day: 90-120 minutes total. Most of it is sending messages, not creating them.

**Time budget assumption:** You have 1-2 hours/day for this. If you have less, double the calendar days.

---

## Day 1 — List building + setup (60-90 min)

| Time | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10 | Read `00-buyer-profile.md` | Buyer picture clear |
| 10-30 | List 20 "warm strangers" — paste into tracking sheet | 20 names in column A |
| 30-45 | List 10 cold targets (search LinkedIn/Reddit/Twitter with queries from buyer profile) | 10 names added |
| 45-60 | Send 5 connection requests on LinkedIn (no message yet) | 5 pending requests |
| 60-75 | Set up Gmail draft with DM 5 (free value) personalized to ONE prospect | 1 draft ready |
| 75-90 | Set up Gumroad or Stripe Payment Link with $99/$199/$399 tiers | Payment live |

**Day 1 success criteria:**
- 30 names in spreadsheet
- 5 connection requests sent
- 1 personalized DM draft ready

---

## Day 2 — First outreach burst (90 min)

| Time | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0-30 | Send DMs to first 10 warm strangers (use DM 1 or DM 2 from `01-cold-dms.md`) | 10 sent |
| 30-60 | Reply to any connection requests accepted from Day 1 | 0-5 replied |
| 60-75 | Post your first community post (start with LinkedIn — see `02-community-posts.md`) | 1 post live |
| 75-90 | Reply to anyone who commented on your post (within 2 hours is critical) | 0-5 replies |

**Day 2 success criteria:**
- 10 DMs sent
- 1 community post live
- 5+ connection requests accepted
- Reply rate starts to emerge (track in `06-tracking-sheet.md`)

**Stop doing:** Don't DM people who don't fit the buyer profile. Don't send to people who won't reply.

---

## Day 3 — Cold outreach + community #2 (90 min)

| Time | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0-30 | Send DMs to 10 cold targets (use DM 6 — question opener — it's lowest friction) | 10 sent |
| 30-50 | Post your second community post (Reddit or Slack) | 1 post live |
| 50-75 | Engage in 2 communities: leave 5 thoughtful comments on other people's posts | 5 comments |
| 75-90 | Follow up on Day 2 non-replies (use the follow-up template in DM 1) | 5 follow-ups |

**Day 3 success criteria:**
- 10 cold DMs sent
- 2 community posts total
- 5 thoughtful comments in other threads
- 1 reply from anyone (hopefully)

**Watch out:** Some people will reply "not interested" — that's fine. Don't argue. Don't take it personally. Move on.

## Day 4 — Follow-ups + community #3 (75 min)

| Time | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0-25 | Follow up on Day 3 non-replies (one follow-up only) | 5 follow-ups |
| 25-45 | Engage in 3 communities — leave comments, answer questions | 5 comments |
| 45-60 | Post your third community post (Twitter thread or Indie Hackers update) | 1 post live |
| 60-75 | Reply to anyone who DMed you from community posts | 0-3 replies |

**Day 4 success criteria:**
- 15 follow-ups sent total
- 3 community posts total
- 10 thoughtful comments total

---

## Day 5 — Calls/discovery sessions (90 min)

| Time | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0-15 | Send DMs to last 10 cold targets | 10 sent |
| 15-60 | Run 30-min discovery calls with anyone who expressed interest (3-5 calls) | 1-3 calls done |
| 60-90 | Engage in 2 communities — daily comment ritual | 5 comments |

**Discovery call structure (30 min):**
- 5 min: "Tell me about your LinkedIn situation"
- 10 min: "What have you tried? What worked?"
- 10 min: "Here's what I'd do for you" (pitch — use `05-founder-pricing.md`)
- 5 min: "Any questions? Want to try month 1?"

**If they say yes:** Send the founder pricing link immediately. Don't wait. Don't send a "thinking about it" follow-up.

**If they say "let me think":** "Totally fair. Here's a guide with 5 things to think about." (Build this guide later if it becomes a common request.)

| Time | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0-30 | Send founder pricing offer to every discovery call who didn't commit on the call | 1-3 offers |
| 30-45 | Follow up on any pending DMs from Days 2-5 | 5 follow-ups |
| 45-60 | One community post or engagement batch | 1 post or 5 comments |

**Day 6 success criteria:**
- 1-3 paying clients (or pending)
- 30+ total DMs sent
- 3+ community posts live
- 5+ calls completed

## Day 7 — Onboarding + retrospective (90 min)

| Time | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0-30 | Onboard paying clients (use the onboarding form in `prompts/README.md`) | Voice profile started |
| 30-45 | Send the first 3 posts to your new client using the prompt system | First posts delivered |
| 45-75 | Retrospective: what worked, what didn't, what's the next experiment | Notes saved |
| 75-90 | Plan week 2 (scale what's working, drop what isn't) | Next week's targets |

**Day 7 success criteria:**
- 1-3 paying clients (real money in)
- 1 testimonial requested
- 1 lesson learned and saved

---

## Day 7-14 — Repeat and scale

By Day 7 you have signal. Days 8-14 are about doubling down:

- Send more DMs (30+/day if it's working)
- Post in more communities
- Refine your offer based on what clients said yes/no to
- Get testimonials from first 3 clients
- Update landing page if pricing/copy needs changes

**The goal:** 5 paying clients by Day 14. If you have 3+ by Day 7, you're on track.

---

## Stop conditions (when to pause and adjust)

- Reply rate < 5% after 30 DMs → your DMs aren't specific enough. Rewrite using DM 5 (free value).
- Discovery calls booked but no conversion → your pitch is off. Read `04-objection-handling.md`.
- Lots of "let me think" replies → your risk reversal isn't strong enough. Read `05-founder-pricing.md`.
- 5+ replies but no calls → ask for the call directly in DM. Add: "Worth 10 min? I'll send a calendar link."

---

## What to track daily

Update your tracking sheet (`06-tracking-sheet.md`) every evening. The 3 numbers that matter:

1. **Reply rate** (replies ÷ DMs sent)
2. **Call rate** (calls ÷ replies)
3. **Conversion rate** (clients ÷ calls)

If reply rate > 15%, your DMs work — just send more.
If reply rate 5-15%, rewrite DMs based on what got replies.
If reply rate < 5%, your targeting is off — go back to buyer profile.

If call rate > 50%, your offer is interesting.
If call rate < 30%, your offer isn't differentiated. Read DM 4 (contrarian) and rewrite your pitch.

If conversion rate > 30%, you have product-market fit. Scale.
If conversion rate < 15%, your pricing or offer has a problem. Re-read founder pricing.
