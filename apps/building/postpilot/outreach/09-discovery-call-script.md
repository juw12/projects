# Discovery Call Script — Word for Word

This is the script for the 30-minute call. Use it word-for-word your first 5 calls. Once you're comfortable, you'll naturally stop reading it.

---

## Before the call (5 minutes)

- Pull up their LinkedIn profile
- Read their last 5-10 posts
- Note 2-3 things to mention specifically
- Have this script open on a second screen
- Have `outreach/05-founder-pricing.md` open in another tab

---

## Opening (5 minutes)

**You:** "Hey [NAME], thanks for taking the time. I'm going to ask you a few questions about your LinkedIn situation, then I'll walk you through how I'd help. Sound good?"

Wait for "yes."

**You:** "Perfect. First question — tell me about your LinkedIn right now. What does it look like?"

**Listen.** Don't interrupt. Take notes.

When they finish:

**You:** "Got it. What's working? What's not?"

**Listen.** They might say things like:
- "I post when I have time, which is never"
- "I post but it doesn't get traction"
- "I used to post daily, then stopped"
- "I have a VA but the posts sound generic"

**You:** "And what have you tried so far?"

**Listen.** This tells you:
- DIY AI (ChatGPT) — they're skeptical of AI but open
- VA or freelancer — they care about quality
- Nothing — they're just starting

---

## Diagnosis (5 minutes)

**You:** "Okay, here's what I'm hearing. [REPEAT BACK IN 2-3 SENTENCES]. Does that match?"

This is critical. They need to feel heard before they'll buy.

Example: "Okay, so you're posting once or twice a month, the posts you do write feel generic even when you spend time on them, and you've tried ChatGPT but it doesn't sound like you. Yeah?"

**Wait for confirmation.**

**You:** "Have you thought about what would change if your LinkedIn was actually consistent for the next 90 days?"

**Listen.** This is the moment they sell themselves on the idea. Their answer tells you their motivation:
- "I'd get more inbound" = they care about leads
- "I'd feel more confident" = they care about brand
- "I'd land more speaking gigs" = they care about visibility
- "I don't know, but I know I should" = they don't know what they want

---

## The pitch (10 minutes)

**You:** "Okay, here's how I'd help with this."

Walk them through what you do — use the post-pilot landing page copy as reference:

1. "I train on 5-10 of your posts to learn your voice."
2. "Every Monday morning, you get 3 ready-to-post drafts."
3. "You approve, edit, or ask for revisions."
4. "By month 2-3, the voice is dialed in so the revisions are minimal."

**Then show them:** Open `prompts/06-sample-client-output.md` (or a real sample for their niche from `outreach/08-preview-posts.md`).

**You:** "Here's an example of what the output looks like. This was for a fractional CMO. The voice is the client's, the structure is mine."

**Listen to their reaction.** If they say "this is good," they're sold. If they say "but does it work for [X niche]," show them another sample.

---

## The close (5 minutes)

**You:** "Here's how the pricing works."

**Open `outreach/05-founder-pricing.md` and read the founder offer:**

"I have a founder offer right now for the first 5 clients — 50% off month 1. So instead of $99, it's $49 for the Starter plan. Or $99 instead of $199 for Growth. After month 1, it goes back to full price. The only thing I ask is a testimonial at the end of the month."

**Then ask directly:**

**You:** "Want to lock in [PLAN] now? I can have your onboarding email sent in 5 minutes."

---

## Handling objections (use `outreach/04-objection-handling.md`)

If they say:

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I need to think about it" | "Totally fair. Here's a free guide with 5 things to think about. I'll send it now. If you decide, just hit reply." |
| "Can I see a sample first?" | "Yes — let me make one in your voice from your last few posts. I'll send it within 24 hours." |
| "I already have a VA" | "Most VAs charge $1-3k/mo and write generic posts. What we do is different. Send me 2 of your VA's posts and 2 of your own — I'll show you the gap." |
| "$99 is a lot for content I haven't seen" | "Agreed. Here's a free draft right now based on your last few posts — no strings." |
| "I need to ask my partner" | "Of course. What would help them say yes? I can put together a one-pager." |

---

## What NOT to do on the call

- Don't talk more than the prospect
- Don't argue with objections
- Don't discount further (the founder offer is already the lowest)
- Don't promise specific outcomes (leads, revenue)
- Don't take notes in a way that looks like you're not listening
- Don't pitch for more than 10 minutes total

---

## After the call (within 1 hour)

**If they said yes:**
- Send the Stripe/Gumroad payment link immediately
- Send the onboarding email from `prompts/05-email-templates.md`
- Add them to your tracking sheet as `Won`

**If they said "let me think":**
- Send the "no worries, here's a guide" reply (see `outreach/04-objection-handling.md`)
- Set a follow-up reminder for 3 days
- Move on. Don't wait.

**If they said no:**
- Send the polite rejection reply (see `outreach/04-objection-handling.md`)
- Don't argue. Don't take it personally.
- Move on.

---

## Practice the script 2-3 times before your first real call

Read it aloud. Have a friend role-play. Record yourself. The first call will feel awkward. By the third call, you'll be natural.

The goal: 30 minutes, prospect feels heard, you've offered a clear next step, you know if it's a yes or no. That's it.
