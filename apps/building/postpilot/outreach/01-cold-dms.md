# File 01 — 10 Cold DM Templates

Each template has: angle tag, when to use, the message (≤120 words), and a follow-up.

**Rules that apply to ALL DMs:**
- First message: no link. Platforms flag spam.
- Personalize the bracketed sections every time. Generic DMs are obvious.
- Reply within 4 hours during business hours. Speed signals seriousness.
- If no reply after 3 days → one follow-up. If still no reply → stop.

---

## DM 1 — Pain-point opener [angle: pain]

**When to use:** They just posted about struggling to write / post / stay consistent.

```
Hey [FIRST NAME] — saw your post about [SPECIFIC THING THEY MENTIONED].

Quick question: how long have you been "meaning to get more consistent" with LinkedIn? 

I ask because I run a service that does exactly this for coaches and consultants. 3 on-brand posts a week, written in your voice. You approve, you post.

No pitch here — genuinely curious about the [X weeks/months] thing. Most of the people I work with say something similar.
```

**Follow-up (3 days, no reply):**
```
No worries if this isn't on your radar. Just dropped a free guide to [1 specific outcome — e.g., "writing a LinkedIn post in 8 minutes flat"]. Happy to send if useful.
```

## DM 2 — Compliment + soft pitch [angle: observation]

**When to use:** They post regularly, but inconsistently.

```
Hey [FIRST NAME] — your post on [SPECIFIC TOPIC] was [SPECIFIC THING — e.g., "the cleanest breakdown of fractional pricing I've read"].

Curious: do you have a system for writing these, or do they just happen when inspiration hits?

I ask because I help a few [THEIR NICHE] do this weekly without thinking about it. Happy to share how if it's useful.
```

**Follow-up:**
```
If it's easier, here's the gist: AI does the draft, you approve. 3 posts a week, 5 minutes of your time. lmk if you want the playbook.
```

## DM 3 — Social proof lead [angle: proof]

**When to use:** When you have at least one client (after Day 3+).

```
Hey [FIRST NAME] — quick context.

I write LinkedIn for a few [NICHE]. One went from 0 to 12k impressions on her best post. Another cut her content time from 4 hours/week to 30 minutes. [EXAMPLE — replace with your real stat when you have one]

If LinkedIn is something you keep meaning to do and not doing, worth a 10-minute call? Not pitching — just curious if the problem is as common as I'm seeing.
```

## DM 4 — Contrarian hook [angle: contrarian]

**When to use:** General cold outreach to coaches who post occasionally.

```
Hot take from working with [NICHE] clients: you don't need to post daily to grow on LinkedIn.

3 thoughtful posts a week, in your voice, beats 7 generic ones written by AI at 2am.

I run a small service that does exactly this. About 15 minutes of your time per week. The rest is on me.

Worth a look?
```

## DM 5 — Free value first [angle: lead magnet]

**When to use:** When you want to qualify interest before pitching.

```
Hey [FIRST NAME] — saw you're in [NICHE]. 

Made you something. Here's a draft LinkedIn post on [THEIR TOPIC] in your voice:

---

[BOLD HOOK LINE]

[3 short paragraphs with a specific angle or story — 150 words max]

[One-line takeaway or question]

---

If this is the kind of thing you'd actually post, I have a service that does this every Monday for [$99-399]/mo. No pressure on this — happy to send a few more drafts to see if it's a fit.
```

**How to make this:** Use the prompt system (`01-weekly-generator.md`) with their public posts. Takes 5 minutes.

## DM 6 — Question opener [angle: curiosity]

**When to use:** When you have no context on them, just a connection.

```
Hey [FIRST NAME] — quick question for you.

If your LinkedIn was actually consistent for the next 90 days, what would that change for your [business/lead flow/inbound]?

I help [NICHE] do exactly this. No idea if it's on your radar — just curious how you'd answer.
```

## DM 7 — Event-trigger [angle: timing]

**When to use:** They just raised prices, launched something, posted a milestone.

```
Hey [FIRST NAME] — saw you [JUST LAUNCHED X / RAISED PRICES / POSTED ABOUT MILESTONE]. Congrats.

Quick observation: most people in your position see 2-3x more inbound when their LinkedIn is consistent through the launch. Right now you're going dark after the announcement.

I run a small service that keeps your LinkedIn active through moments like this — 3 on-brand posts a week, written in your voice, you just approve.

Worth a chat? 10 minutes, no pitch.
```

## DM 8 — Referral ask [angle: warm intro]

**When to use:** You have a warm contact who mentioned someone they know.

```
Hey [FIRST NAME] — [MUTUAL CONNECTION] mentioned you'd be the right person to talk to about LinkedIn stuff.

They said you've been [SPECIFIC THING — e.g., "thinking about getting more consistent"].

I run a small service for [NICHE] — 3 posts a week in your voice, you approve. 10-min intro call worth it?
```

---

## DM 9 — Direct offer with risk reversal [angle: bold]

**When to use:** Confident, after you've sent 10+ lower-friction DMs and tested the offer.

```
Hey [FIRST NAME] — I'll write your LinkedIn for a month. 

3 on-brand posts a week, in your voice. Delivered Monday morning. You approve or edit. Cancel anytime. If your first month doesn't deliver posts you'd actually publish, I refund it.

[$99/mo to start]. No contract. 

10 minutes to onboard. Worth trying?
```

## DM 10 — Voice-mimicry sample [angle: personalized]

**When to use:** You have time to make a real sample (5 minutes). Highest conversion, lowest volume.

```
Hey [FIRST NAME] — I cloned your writing voice from your last 5 LinkedIn posts.

Here's a draft post in your style:

---

[BOLD HOOK LINE]

[3 paragraphs — 150 words]

[One-line takeaway or question]

---

Posted this exact pattern would land well in [NICHE] right now.

If you want, I can do this every week. [$99/mo, 3 posts/week, you approve.]

If not — no worries, the post is yours to use.
```

**Note:** This is your highest-conversion DM. Volume is 5/day max because each takes 5-7 minutes to make. Worth it for the right targets.

## Channel-specific notes

### LinkedIn DMs
- Keep under 300 chars for first message (better open rate)
- Connection request → wait for accept → THEN send DM (not before)
- Don't include links in first message

### Twitter/X DMs
- Be very short. 1-2 sentences max.
- Many coaches check DMs less than LinkedIn. LinkedIn first if you have a choice.

### Email (cold)
- Subject line matters more than body. Use: "saw your [POST]" or "quick question about [NICHE]"
- 3 paragraphs max
- Plain text > fancy templates (deliverability)

### Reddit
- NEVER pitch in DMs without context. Comment on their posts first.
- Build karma in the sub before any commercial intent.
- "I do this for a living" + relevant link = OK. "Buy my course" = banned.
