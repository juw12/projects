# File 05 — Founder Pricing

The "first 5 clients" offer. Designed to get real testimonials and case studies fast.

---

## The offer

**50% off month 1.** Same plan, same deliverables, same quality.

| Plan | Regular | Founder | You Get |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter (3 posts/wk) | $99/mo | **$49/mo** | Testimonial + permission to use name |
| Growth (5 posts + replies) | $199/mo | **$99/mo** | Testimonial + 1 case study interview (15 min) |
| Scale (daily + strategy) | $399/mo | **$199/mo** | Testimonial + case study + 1 LinkedIn recommendation |

The price returns to full after month 1. They can cancel anytime.

**Why this works:**
- Cheap enough that they say yes without much deliberation
- Just expensive enough that they take it seriously
- Real testimonials → unlocks future full-price sales
- 5 clients at $49-199 = $245-995 in your first month, plus 5 references

---

## What you ASK in return

### From every founder client
1. **Testimonial** (3-5 sentences) — you write the first draft based on a 10-min call
2. **Permission to use their name** on your landing page
3. **One quick check-in** at week 2 (5 min) — what worked, what didn't

### From Growth/Scale founder clients (additional)
4. **Case study interview** (15 min) — recorded, transcribed, turned into a story
5. **LinkedIn recommendation** from them to you (15-second favor)

That's it. Don't ask for referrals. Don't ask for social posts. Don't ask for "before/after metrics" — those take months to materialize.

---

## The pitch (send after a discovery call or DM reply)

Use this in DMs (≤80 words) or in the email after a call.

```
Hey [NAME] — wanted to send the founder offer:

50% off month 1, no contract.

- Starter (3 posts/wk): $49 instead of $99
- Growth (5 posts + replies): $99 instead of $199
- Scale (daily + strategy): $199 instead of $399

What I ask in return:
- A 5-sentence testimonial at the end of month 1
- Permission to use your name on my site

Onboarding takes 10 minutes. First posts land next Monday.

Want to lock in [PLAN]?
```

---

## When to send this offer

Send the founder pricing:
- After a discovery call (yes/no moment)
- After someone replies to a DM 5 (free value) with "this is great, how do I sign up?"
- After someone replies to a DM 9 (direct offer) saying "tell me more"

Don't send it:
- As a first message (too forward)
- To someone who hasn't replied to anything (they're not warm enough)
- To people who haven't expressed any interest (looks desperate)

---

## Handling the founder pricing conversation

If they say "yes" — send the Stripe Payment Link immediately. Don't wait.

If they say "let me think" — let them. Follow up once in 48 hours. If no reply, move on.

If they say "$49 is still a lot" — they're not your customer. Send the polite "no worries, here's a guide" reply.

If they say "I want to do this for 6 months at founder pricing" — counter: "How about month 1 at founder price, months 2-3 at 25% off, then full price?" You can offer this. Don't give 6 months at 50% off.

---

## After they say yes — onboarding (10 minutes)

1. Send the onboarding form (in `prompts/README.md`)
2. Collect: niche, audience, samples, topics, banned, personal details
3. Run File 00 (voice calibration) in Google AI Studio
4. Save their voice profile as `[clientname]-voice.md`
5. Tell them: "First 3 posts land Monday at 9am [YOUR TIMEZONE]. Reply with edits or hit approve."

**That's it.** You're delivering the service.

---

## When to stop offering founder pricing

After 5 clients. Then it's done. Public price goes back to $99/$199/$399.

If you're at 5 clients and need more, raise prices or change the offer slightly — never run "founder pricing" forever or it stops meaning anything.

---

## Tracking founder clients

In your tracking sheet, mark founder clients with a `FOUNDER` tag. After all 5, change the offer. Don't double-count.
