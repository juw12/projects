# PostPilot — First 7 Days

You've shipped the product. Now you need customers. This folder is your day-by-day playbook.

## The goal

**5 paying clients in 14 days.** That's the target. Not 50, not "build an audience." Five humans who paid you money to use what you built.

## How the next 7 days look

| Day | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | Identify 20 "warm strangers" + 10 cold targets | Spreadsheet filled in |
| **Day 2** | Send 20 DMs (warm) + post in 1 community | 20 sent, 1 post live |
| **Day 3** | Send 10 cold DMs + post in 1 more community | 10 sent, 2 posts live |
| **Day 4** | Follow up on non-replies from Day 2 + post in 1 more community | 15 follow-ups, 3 posts live |
| **Day 5** | Follow up on non-replies from Day 3 + engage in 3 communities | Follow-ups, comments on 10 posts |
| **Day 6** | Calls/discovery sessions with anyone who replied | 1-3 calls |
| **Day 7** | Send "founder pricing" offers + onboarding | 1-3 paying clients |

## What success looks like at Day 7

- 30+ DMs sent
- 3+ community posts live
- 5+ discovery calls completed
- 1-3 paying clients
- 2 testimonials collected
- Refined version of the offer based on what you learned

If you're at 0 clients by Day 7 — see `WHAT-IF-NOTHING-HAPPENS.md` (in this folder).

## What success looks like at Day 14

- 5 paying clients
- 4-5 testimonials
- Real data on what the offer actually needs to be
- Decision point: scale to 50 clients, or stay small and raise prices?

## Day 1 — your first hour

1. Open `00-buyer-profile.md`. Read it.
2. Open your phone contacts / LinkedIn connections. List 20 people who fit the "warm stranger" profile.
3. Open a new Google Sheet. Paste the template from `06-tracking-sheet.md`.
4. Stop. That's your first hour.

The rest of Day 1 is filling in the columns.

Go.
