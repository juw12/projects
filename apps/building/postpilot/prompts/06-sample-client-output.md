# Weekly Posts for Jordan Hayes — 2026-11-03

## POST 1
**I lost my biggest client last year. Best thing that happened to my agency.**

She was a $2M skincare brand. 18-month engagement. $14k/month.

She was also the worst fit I'd ever taken.

Too small. Too early. Founder wasn't ready for the work. I'd been telling myself "this is fine" for 9 months.

When she left, I had 14 days of panic. Then I realized: the slot she'd been occupying was the slot a $4M brand was waiting to fill.

I filled it in 11 days. At $22k/month.

The math: I lost $14k. I gained $22k. Net up 57%. Plus 30 hours a month I was no longer spending on the wrong fit.

📌 The cost of a bad client isn't the revenue you lose. It's the better client you don't take.

---

## POST 2
**Your "first marketing hire" trap.**

Founders ask me constantly: "When do I hire my first marketer?"

The honest answer: never. Until you can answer this question:

"What's the one thing this person will be uniquely good at that you can't?"

If you don't know, you're hiring to fill a role, not a gap.

The first marketing hire I ever recommended was a retention marketer for a $3M brand. The founder wanted a "growth person." But she had no retention. Churn was eating the growth.

Hire the gap. Not the title.

📌 If your job description says "growth marketer," you don't know what you need.

---

## POST 3
**The positioning mistake 90% of DTC founders make.**

They position by category. "We're the sustainable skincare brand." "We're the affordable luxury jewelry brand."

Category positioning is the same as saying "we're one of many."

The brands that break $5M position by enemy. "We're not the [X] brand. We're the [Y] brand."

Enemy positioning works because it implies you made a choice. You decided what you're NOT.

Glossier positioned against the heavily made-up look of the 2010s. Liquid Death positioned against "premium" water brands. Both went from zero to $100M+.

If your "About" page could be rewritten by any of your competitors by swapping 2 words, you don't have positioning.

❌ Category is a seat at the table. Enemy is the whole table.
