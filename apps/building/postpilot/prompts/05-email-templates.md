# Email Templates — Client Lifecycle

Three templates for the most common client touchpoints. Copy-paste into Gmail (use "Save as template" so they're one click away).

---

## Email 1 — Welcome + Onboarding (sent within 5 min of payment)

**Subject:** Welcome to PostPilot — let's get your voice dialed in

```
Hey [CLIENT NAME],

Welcome to PostPilot. Excited to have you.

Here's what happens next:

**Today (10 minutes from you):**
Answer these 7 questions — the better your answers, the better your posts:

1. What's your niche? (e.g., "Executive coaching for SaaS founders")
2. Who's your ideal client? (e.g., "VP Sales at Series A startups")
3. Paste 5-10 of your past LinkedIn posts here (or posts you admire and wish you'd written)
4. What 3-5 topics do you want to be known for?
5. Any topics, phrases, or stories you NEVER want to see in your posts?
6. Anything specific to weave in? (years of experience, signature story, frameworks you teach, company name, etc.)
7. One sentence: what should someone think after reading 10 of your posts?

**This week (0 minutes from you):**
I'll train on your samples and build your Voice Profile.

**Next Monday at 9am [YOUR TIMEZONE]:**
You get your first 3 posts in your inbox.

**The loop:**
- Monday: you receive 3 drafts
- You read, edit, approve
- Hit reply with edits or "ship it"
- Wednesday at 9am: revised drafts back (if needed)
- Friday: you post them

Cancel anytime. First month refund if you're not happy — no questions, no forms.

Reply to this email with your answers when ready.

Talk soon,
Sisyphus Operator

P.S. If you want to schedule a 10-min kickoff call to walk through the onboarding together, here's my calendar: [CALENDAR LINK]
```

## Email 2 — Weekly Delivery (every Monday)

**Subject:** Your 3 posts for this week

```
Hey [CLIENT NAME],

3 posts ready for you. Read in 5 minutes, edit as little or as much as you want.

---

**POST 1 — [TOPIC]**

[BOLD HOOK]

[Body — 150-220 words]

[CTA or question]

*Strategic angle: [1 line — why this post, what it positions you as]*

---

**POST 2 — [TOPIC]**

[same structure]

---

**POST 3 — [TOPIC]**

[same structure]

---

**This week's strategy:**
- Post 1 → [angle, recommended day]
- Post 2 → [angle, recommended day]
- Post 3 → [angle, recommended day]

**Recommended posting schedule:**
- Monday afternoon: Post 1
- Wednesday morning: Post 2
- Friday morning: Post 3

Reply with edits or "ship it" by Friday and you're done for the week.

If anything feels off-voice, flag it. I'll add it to your Voice Profile so next week is closer.

Talk Friday,
Sisyphus Operator
```

## Email 3 — Testimonial Request (end of month 1)

**Subject:** Quick favor — 5 sentences about PostPilot?

**Send timing:** Day 28-30 of month 1. Don't wait until after they've cancelled.

```
Hey [CLIENT NAME],

We're at the end of month 1 — that's when I'd love a quick testimonial if you've gotten value.

If you can spare 5 minutes, here's what would help most:

1. **What was the situation before PostPilot?** (2 sentences — what was hard about LinkedIn before)
2. **What changed in month 1?** (2 sentences — what's different now)
3. **Who would you recommend PostPilot to?** (1 sentence — what type of coach/consultant would benefit most)

If writing isn't your thing, hop on a 10-min call with me and I'll write the draft based on what you say. Just reply with a time that works.

Either way — thanks for trusting me with your LinkedIn. The first month is the hardest. Month 2 onward is when the voice really locks in.

Sisyphus Operator

P.S. If you ever want to stop, just reply "cancel" — no forms, no friction. Thanks for trying.
```

## Email 4 — Cancellation Confirmation (when someone cancels)

**Subject:** Cancelled — thanks for trying PostPilot

```
Hey [CLIENT NAME],

You're all set — your subscription is cancelled. No charges going forward.

You keep everything we already made — it's yours.

If you have a minute, I'd love to know what didn't work. Honest feedback helps me make this better. Just hit reply with one sentence.

If circumstances change, the door's open. You can re-subscribe anytime.

Thanks for giving it a shot.

Sisyphus Operator
```

## Email 5 — Re-engagement (60 days after cancellation)

**Subject:** Still thinking about LinkedIn?

```
Hey [CLIENT NAME],

Saw you cancelled PostPilot a couple months back.

No pitch here. Just curious: are you still posting on LinkedIn, or did it fall off?

If it's the second one, I have one new thing that might help — happy to share if useful.

If it's the first, nice work.

Either way, no hard feelings. Hope the business is going well.

Sisyphus Operator
```

**Note:** Don't send this if they were angry about something. Use it only for "it just wasn't the right time" cancellations.

## How to save these as Gmail templates

1. Compose a new email in Gmail
2. Paste the template
3. Replace `[BRACKETED PLACEHOLDERS]` with your actual info
4. Click the 3 dots menu → "Save draft as template" → name it
5. When you need it: Compose → 3 dots → "Templates" → pick it

(Or use a Gmail extension like "Templates" if you send many of these — saves more time.)
