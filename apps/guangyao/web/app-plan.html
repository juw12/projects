01 / PRODUCT SCOPE

What this deliverable is—and is not.

Specified by you

  • Accounts, profiles, learning levels, classes, videos, materials, quizzes, and progression.
  • Certificates and a shareable achievement portfolio.
  • Points, prize redemption, invitations, and affiliate earnings.
  • Organization introduction, online/offline schedules, teachers, and social activities.
  • Role-restricted activity creation and access.
  • A transparent “good deeds vault,” donations, achievements, and leaderboards.
  • Friends, group chats, polls, meetings, recordings, summaries, and history.

Assumptions for this concept

  • Web first, with desktop and mobile layouts. Native apps can follow.
  • Traditional Chinese interface; English planning document. Additional languages remain open.
  • “光耀同行” is a proposed product name, not an approved organizational brand.
  • Three illustrative course levels; three units and a two-question quiz per course.
  • Initial demonstration points: 320. First course completion reward: 100. These are not approved policy.
  • Sample schedule: November 2026, Taipei time. All teachers, courses, activities, balances, and messages are illustrative.
  • Default private profile/portfolio; voluntary learning leaderboard.
The linked reference is a starting point for authorized organization content, not proof of endorsement. The prototype deliberately avoids inventing official history, doctrine, endorsements, contact details, accreditation, or financial authorization. User-supplied 發一光耀 reference ↗

Prototype operation

Open the prototype in a current browser. It works as a local web project without a build step. Installation and offline caching require localhost or an HTTPS deployment; a direct file opening remains a limited preview. Since v4 it opens with a welcome screen: sign in with a free Puter account (progress syncs) or continue as a guest (local only). Use the top-right account button to change display name, sign out, or reset local progress. Staff work happens in the separate admin console.

Changes are saved to this browser’s local storage where supported. They are not shared with other people. Do not enter real passwords, personal information, meeting secrets, bank details, or card data. Clearing browser data or moving to another origin may reset or separate the demo state.

Real integrations are not implemented: secure authentication, multi-user messaging, actual video playback, payment collection, cloud publication, credential verification, affiliate payouts, live meetings, recordings, or AI summaries.

04 / USER JOURNEYS & RULES

Connect the screens with dependable rules.

A. Learn → progress → share

Discover and enroll

Check prerequisites and explain why a class is locked.

Learn and assess

Complete required units and the approved assessment. Failed attempts show feedback without granting completion.

Award once

Record completion, grant an idempotent points transaction, issue an authorized credential, and unlock eligible classes.

Share by choice

Select public achievements. A credential may remain valid even if its owner hides their portfolio; the public verifier must reveal only approved information.

B. Discover → register → participate

Check eligibility

Server filters visibility, membership, level, role, capacity, and schedule.

Reserve or waitlist

Reserve atomically; show confirmed status and cancellation policy.

Prepare and join

Send time-zone-aware reminders and secure meeting access, or verified offline logistics.

Record actual participation

Registration is not proof of attendance. Award attendance milestones only after authorized verification.

C. Support → reconcile → report

Informed contribution

Identify the legal recipient, project, fund restrictions, fees, privacy, and refund rules.

Confirm payment

Only a verified provider webhook changes payment status; browser success redirects are not authoritative.

Allocate and spend

Track approved budgets separately from cash movement; require review and redacted supporting documents.

Publish reconciled updates

Show dated summaries, changes, fees, refunds, transfers, and unresolved reconciliation issues. Do not equate donor wealth with achievement.

D. Earn → redeem / refer → settle

Earn participation points

One source transaction per approved achievement; no points from arbitrary client-side input.

Redeem with certainty

Balance and inventory checked in one transaction. Cancellation creates a reversal rather than deleting history.

Track referrals separately

Record opt-in attribution, a qualifying action, and fraud review. No multi-level recruitment or donation commissions.

Settle only after approval

Commission holds, payable amounts, paid amounts, and reversals remain distinct.

05 / PRODUCTION BUILD

A realistic architecture, not a single giant page.

The delivered prototype is intentionally dependency-free HTML/CSS/JavaScript. A production version needs secure services. The following is a proposed architecture—not a finalized vendor selection.

Application layer

Responsive web client with accessible design system, Traditional Chinese localization, public pages, authenticated learning/community workspace, and a separate staff workspace.

Candidate: TypeScript with a mature web framework. Use a PWA where useful; do not assume offline access to protected media.

Data & identity

Server API, managed identity, PostgreSQL, scoped authorization, transactional ledgers, object storage with signed URLs, search, and durable background jobs.

Candidate: managed Postgres/Auth/Storage or equivalent infrastructure. Policies must be tested, not inferred from UI behavior.

External services

Licensed video hosting, transactional notifications, approved payment provider, live meeting/recording provider, transcription, and optional AI summaries.

Choose vendors only after region, privacy, cost, availability, retention, charity support, and API requirements are confirmed.

Core data entities

Identity: users, private profiles, public profiles, role assignments, scoped permissions, consent records, audit events.

Learning: courses and versions, prerequisites, lessons, resources, enrollments, progress events, assessments, attempts, completions, credentials, revocations.

Events & community: activities, hosts, access policies, registrations, attendance, groups, memberships, messages, friendships, polls, votes, reports, moderation actions.

Meetings: provider sessions, participant authorization, recording consents, recordings, transcript segments, summary versions, retention/deletion tasks.

Rewards: point ledger, earning rules, reward inventory, reservations, redemptions, fulfillment, reversals.

Giving: projects, budgets, donation intents, provider events, financial entries, allocations, expenses, evidence, approvals, refunds, reconciliation snapshots.

Referral: campaign terms, referral codes, consent/attribution events, qualifying conversions, commission entries, payout batches, reversals.

Sensitive context: religious participation, private conversations, and financial contribution records deserve strong privacy controls. Minimize collection, restrict purpose and access, define retention, offer deletion/export where applicable, and complete local legal review before public launch. Do not claim regulatory compliance or tax deductibility from this prototype.

Non-negotiable delivery work

Security and operational safety
  • Threat modeling; per-resource authorization; staff MFA; signed and idempotent webhooks.
  • Rate limiting, abuse detection, upload validation/malware handling, audit logging, and credential revocation.
  • Encryption in transit/at rest, secret management, backups with restore drills, monitoring, and incident response.
  • Third-party data-processing agreements and region/retention review before transmitting recordings or personal information.
Accessibility and language

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with a dedicated audit: keyboard navigation, dialog focus, readable contrast, captioned media, text alternatives, meaningful errors, adequate tap targets, and accessible quizzes. Do not claim conformance without verification. Decide Traditional/Simplified Chinese and English scope before content production.

Scale and reliability

Confirm user count, concurrent streams, chat volume, storage, monthly meeting hours, and uptime needs. Design transactional limits and job retries; load-test registration openings, assessments, payment webhooks, and certificate issuance. No performance or hosting-cost guarantee has been estimated.

07 / DECISIONS BEFORE A LIVE BUILD

Questions that materially change implementation.

Organization & learning

  1. Is this an official authorized platform or an independent concept? Who approves the name, copy, and certificates?
  2. Who are the learners, in which countries, and are minors included?
  3. Which languages are needed first?
  4. What are the actual course levels, prerequisites, teachers, completion rules, and paid/free enrollment terms?
  5. What does a certificate officially attest to, and who may issue/revoke it?

Operations & finance

  1. Which roles can create, approve, and view each activity? Does “higher rank” refer to learning, organizational status, or delegated permission?
  2. Who legally receives donations, and what transparency/receipt obligations apply?
  3. Are referral rewards non-cash points, cash commissions on eligible courses, or both?
  4. Who fulfills prizes, handles support, moderates groups, and approves financial records?
  5. What are the budget, target launch, expected usage, preferred meeting provider, and recording retention rules?
08 / PROTOTYPE VALIDATION & DEMO TOUR

Try the experience end to end.

  1. Learning: open the first course. Unit 1 starts completed as seeded demo progress. Preview and complete units 2 and 3, then answer the quiz. The correct choices reflect listening respectfully and protecting privacy.
  2. Progression: check that the next course unlocks, the balance rises once, and the sample certificate appears under achievements. Open its information panel and try the download.
  3. Activities: open a schedule item, register, filter to your registrations, and cancel. Compare list and month views.
  4. Roles: switch to organizer or admin in the account dialog. Create an activity restricted to staff. Switch back to member and confirm the restricted activity is hidden.
  5. Community: write a nonsensitive sample message, create a local group, send a friend invitation, vote in the sample poll, and choose whether to appear in the leaderboard.
  6. Rewards: redeem the notebook; confirm the points ledger, balance, stock, and redemption record update together.
  7. Giving: inspect a project’s sample ledger, submit an explicitly simulated contribution, and view the private record. As admin, test the simulated review. No money changes hands.
  8. Meetings: preview the room, open a past-session summary, and download the sample recap. No microphone/camera access or recording occurs.
  9. Persistence: reload in the same browser to check retained local state. Use account → reset prototype to restore the initial demo.
Version 2 validation completed: 16 grouped regression checks passed in Chromium and WebKit, including 72 route-layout checks at 1440, 768 and 390 pixels. The test suite covers course completion, failed assessments, unlocking, sample certificates, registration and cancellation, restricted organizer content, escaped chat, voting, reward redemption, simulated donations, local persistence, logout/login/reset, mobile drawer behavior and install instructions. Additional Chromium checks cover corrupted data, legacy migration, concurrent-tab redemption, stale registration, manifest installability diagnostics, project-subpath scope, offline reload and consented service-worker updates. No uncaught application errors occurred in the core browser runs.

Still unverified: physical iPhone/Android installation, screen-reader conformance, native-store packaging, operating-system download dialogs, real multi-user services, backend security, payments, organizational approval and official credential validity. Browser WebKit tests are not equivalent to physical iOS testing. The prior version also exercised all three course levels and generated sample document contents.
09 / WEB + MOBILE UPGRADE

A more robust frontend, ready for a confirmed deployment.

What improved

  • Installable PWA manifest and icons, standalone presentation, bottom navigation, safe areas, stronger small-text contrast and touch-sized mobile controls.
  • Scoped local records, recognized legacy-data migration, defensive shape/identifier validation and derived demo balances.
  • Serialized cross-tab actions with Web Locks, storage-change notifications, explicit registration intent and stale-redemption feedback.

  • Public-shell-only offline caching, project-directory-safe paths and user-approved version updates.

What has not been published

A manual GitHub Pages deployment workflow, Obsidian-ready project note, portfolio case-study draft and reproducible browser tests are prepared locally.

Source integration targets are juwpegatron/projects and juwpegatron/portfolio. This app is prepared for the existing GitHub Pages build pipeline. The Obsidian vault remains unconfirmed; publication status depends on a verified authenticated push and a successful Pages deployment, not on the existence of this frontend.

Real accounts, media, payments, chat, meetings and credential verification still need approved backend services. This frontend is not a live institutional platform or a native App Store application.

Mobile installation needs an HTTPS deployment accessible from the phone. The Mac's localhost preview is local to the Mac. Data remains on each browser/installation; it does not synchronize between desktop and mobile. Offline caching excludes private server data.

11 / V4 ACCOUNTS, ADMIN & AI

From prototype to something people can use.

Version 4 adds real identity, real publishing and a real AI companion without running a server of our own.

Learners

  • Sign in with Puter (free account): progress, cards, journal and registrations sync across devices and merge without loss.
  • Continue as guest: everything stays on the device; the app never contacts Puter.
  • First-visit tour and an in-app 使用指南 with FAQ and privacy notes.

Admins

  • admin.html: GitHub collaborators log in with a token (session-only by default).
  • Upload handouts (≤ 20 MB), build lessons with text, YouTube, quiz, reflection and flashcards, publish events and an announcement.
  • Every change is a commit in the public content repository—history, review and rollback for free.

AI study companion 光光

  • Streamed answers for signed-in learners, grounded in course text and official material notes.
  • Hints before answers, honest “not covered”, AI-made flashcards, chat history.
  • Offline-only switch and a privacy toggle for learner context; guests use the on-device tutor.
Still demonstrations: points and rewards, giving, community chat, meetings and referrals. The content repository is public—never upload personal or internal information.
