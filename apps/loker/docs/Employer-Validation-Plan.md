# Loker — Employer Validation Plan (10 interviews)

**Purpose:** test whether real SME employers would **use and pay** for Loker's core promise (verified, direct-chat hiring) — *before* building more product.
**Rule:** this is the cheapest possible test. No more code needed to run it.

---

## 1. Hypothesis

> F&B SMEs in one Jakarta area struggle to hire front-line staff (baristas, cashiers, servers) **quickly and safely**, and would pay a small recurring fee (or per hire) for a tool that delivers **responsive, verified** candidates they can **chat with directly** — no brokers/HR.

If that's false, the business is not there — and we learn it in ~10 days for ~zero cost.

## 2. The niche (pick ONE — do not broaden yet)

- **Segment:** F&B (cafés, warungs, restaurants, cloud kitchens).
- **Geography:** ONE area (e.g. Jakarta Selatan — Kemang / Fatmawati / Senopati).
- **Target person:** the **decision-maker** (owner or ops manager), *not* an HR admin.
- **Sample:** 10 employers — a mix of:
  - 5 who currently post on JobStreet / Glints / Instagram
  - 5 who hire only via word-of-mouth / WhatsApp / walk-ins

## 3. How to reach them

1. **Warm** (fastest): your network, alumni, suppliers, local business WhatsApp/Facebook groups, community events (*kopdar*).
2. **Cold:** walk into ~15–20 nearby cafés at **off-peak hours** (10–11 AM or 2–4 PM); or DM the business Instagram account.

## 4. Outreach message

**Bahasa Indonesia (WhatsApp / IG DM):**
> Halo [Nama]! Saya [Nama]. Saya sedang membuat alat bantu untuk pemilik usaha F&B di Jakarta supaya bisa cari karyawan (barista, kasir, pelayan) lebih cepat — kandidatnya sudah **diverifikasi**, jadi bisa **chat langsung** tanpa calo atau HR. Boleh minta waktu 15 menit untuk tanya-tanya soal cara Bapak/Ibu hire sekarang? Saya tidak jualan, hanya minta masukan. Terima kasih!

**English:**
> Hi [Name]! I'm [Name]. I'm building a tool for F&B owners in Jakarta to hire staff (baristas, cashiers, servers) faster — **verified** candidates you can **chat with directly**, no brokers or HR. Could I have 15 minutes to ask how you hire today? Not selling — just want your input. Thanks!

## 5. Interview script (10–15 min)

**Rules:** ask about **past/current behavior**, never "would you use my app?" first. Shut up and let them talk. Take notes verbatim.

1. Tell me about the **last time** you hired a barista/cashier/server. How did you find them, and how long did it take?
2. How many people applied? How many actually showed up / were any good?
3. What went wrong most often — **no-shows, unqualified, scams, or fast quits**?
4. How much **time per week** do you spend on hiring when you have a vacancy?
5. What do you **currently pay** to hire? (JobStreet/Glints credits, *calo* / brokers, referral bonuses, ads.)
6. Have you ever had a bad experience — a fake applicant, a scam, a broker who charged you? *(trust wedge)*
7. When you have a vacancy, **who decides and how fast** do you need someone?
8. If I could show you **5 pre-verified candidates you can chat with the same day**, what would that be **worth** per hire / per month?
9. What would make you **not trust** a candidate list? *(verification requirements)*
10. If this existed today, **would you pay** for it? If not, what's missing?

## 6. Signals to record (buying vs polite)

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Strong** | Asks "how much?", asks to be told at launch, names a **specific vacancy right now**, offers a **deposit / pilot fee**, asks how fast onboarding works |
| 🟡 **Medium** | "Cool", "interesting", "let me know" — but no commitment, no money |
| ❌ **Weak** | "We're fine", "maybe later", won't share current spend |

## 7. Scoring & decision (after 10)

Count who would **PAY** (deposit / prepay / pilot fee) — not who "likes it".

- **≥ 3 ready-to-pay + shared clear pain** → build the pilot (Phase 1: one niche).
- **1–2** → narrow further (one specific role or street) and re-test.
- **0 willing to pay but pain is real** → the model/pricing is wrong; try the fintech / adjacent angle or pivot.
- **Pain not shared** → stop. It isn't a business.

## 8. Logistics

- **Target:** 10 interviews within 7–10 days.
- Book 15-min slots; schedule F&B-friendly (10–11 AM or 2–4 PM, off-peak).
- Track one row per employer: name · role · business · channel · Q1–Q10 answers · buying signal (✅/🟡/❌) · price quoted.
- Baseline to beat: the **time-to-hire** and **cost-per-hire** each employer reports today.

## 9. Definition of success

**3+ employers willing to pay real money** to pilot a verified, direct-chat hiring tool for one role in one area.
That — not more app features — is the signal to proceed to Phase 1.

---

*This plan deliberately avoids asking "do you like my app?" and avoids hypotheticals. It measures money and past behavior, the only reliable signals at this stage.*
