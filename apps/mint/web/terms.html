What Mint is

Mint is a studio and marketplace for spreadsheet templates, run by Juwen Lie (Batam, Indonesia) as a personal project. Sellers list templates and are paid directly by buyers, either on the seller’s own payment link or through the seller’s own Midtrans, Xendit or Stripe account. Mint does not process payments, hold money or act as a party to the sale.

Buying

  • Ordering a paid template creates a reference (like M-7K3QXA). If the seller has automatic payments on, you pay on their checkout and the file unlocks as soon as the provider confirms payment. Otherwise pay the seller’s link with the reference in the payment note; the seller confirms and the file unlocks in your Library.
  • Orders not confirmed within 14 days expire. Refunds, disputes and payment problems are between you and the seller; use the seller’s checkout support.
  • Previews of paid templates show sample results only; formulas and logic come with the purchased file.

Selling

  • Only sell templates you made or have the right to sell. You are responsible for your listing, your payment link, delivering what you describe and any taxes.
  • Confirm orders only after the payment has really arrived, and pay affiliate commissions you owe promptly to the details they provide.
  • If you connect a Midtrans, Xendit or Stripe account, you are the merchant: that provider’s terms, fees, refunds and tax rules apply to you. Use a key that can only create checkouts and read payment status where the provider allows it.
  • Listings reported by several different users are hidden automatically until reviewed.

Affiliates

Affiliates earn the commission the seller sets on sales made through their link within 30 days. Sellers pay commissions directly; Mint tracks them but does not guarantee payment. Disclose affiliate links where the law or platform requires it.

Not allowed

  • Copied or stolen templates, misleading listings, malware or harmful formulas, spam, hateful or illegal content.
  • Fake orders, fake reviews, self-referrals or abusing rate limits and reports.

Accounts or listings that break these rules may be removed. You can delete your account at any time (how).

Age

You must be at least 13 to use Mint. If you are under 18, buy or sell only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission.
