Delete your Mint account

Mint (by Juwen Lie) lets you delete your account at any time, from the app or from this page. You do not need the app installed.

Option 1 — in the app

  1. Open Mint and sign in.
  2. Go to Account and scroll to Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

Option 2 — right here

Sign in with the email and password of the account you want to delete. This cannot be undone.

Option 3 — by email

Forgot your password and lost your recovery code? Email juwen12006@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Mint account deletion”. Requests are handled within 14 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login: email, name, password hash and recovery-code hash (replaced with an anonymous placeholder).
  • Your drafts, affiliate links, payout details, connected payment-provider keys, push-notification subscriptions and all sign-in sessions.
  • Your listings are taken off the market and their payment links removed. Pending orders are cancelled.

Deletion is immediate. Encrypted platform backups (Cloudflare) roll over within 30 days.

What is kept, and why

  • Completed order records (reference, template, price, date) stay so the other party keeps their purchase or sales history. Your name on them shows as “Deleted user”.
  • Reviews you wrote stay, shown as “Deleted user”.
  • Templates you bought are no longer accessible to you after deletion; download any files you want to keep first.

See the Privacy policy for everything Mint stores.
