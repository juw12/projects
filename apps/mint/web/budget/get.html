Mint Open in browser

Mint is free on every device.

One app for your shared budgets and your spreadsheet templates: download it for Windows, Mac or Android, put it on your iPhone home screen, or use it in any browser. Invite links work across all of them, so a family on different devices can share one budget.

🪟 Windows 10 & 11 Free

  1. Tap Download for Windows and open Mint-Windows-Setup.exe.
  2. Press Install (no admin rights needed). If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info → Run anyway: the app is free and not code-signed yet.
  3. Open Mint from the Start menu. The first time, pick your language and region.
Download for Windows No install: portable .zip

🍏 Mac Free

  1. Download the version for your Mac's chip (Apple menu → About This Mac shows Chip for Apple or Processor for Intel).
  2. Open the .dmg and drag Mint to Applications.
  3. The first time, macOS blocks it because it isn't notarized yet: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
Apple chip (M1 and newer) Intel chip

🍎 iPhone & iPad Free

  1. Open juw12.github.io/projects/mint in Safari.
  2. Tap Share , then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Mint from your home screen. It runs full-screen like any app, and your budgets keep working offline.
Open Mint in Safari

You're already using Mint from the home screen. 🎉

An App Store listing needs a paid Apple Developer account, so the home-screen app is the iPhone version for now.

🤖 Android Free

  1. Tap Download for Android below (a signed .apk, about 3 MB).
  2. Open the file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then open Mint and pick your language and region. New features arrive automatically.
Download for Android

The Android build is on its way. Until then, open Mint in Chrome and tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen. It installs as an app with the same features.

In the app, backups, CSV files and result cards open the share sheet, so you can save them to Files or Drive.

On your computer?

Scan with your phone's camera to open this page there and install Mint in a few taps.

No account needed for budgets, no ads, no subscription. Mint is a free portfolio project by Juwen Lie.
