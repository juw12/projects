Mint Open in browser

Mint is free on every device.

One app for your shared budgets and your spreadsheet templates: use it on the web, put it on your iPhone home screen, or install it on Android. Invite links work across all of them, so a couple on iPhone and Android can share one budget.

🍎 iPhone & iPad Free

  1. Open juw12.github.io/projects/mint in Safari.
  2. Tap Share , then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Mint from your home screen. It runs full-screen like any app, and your budgets keep working offline.
Open Mint in Safari

You're already using Mint from the home screen. 🎉

An App Store listing needs a paid Apple Developer account, so the home-screen app is the iPhone version for now.

🤖 Android Free

  1. Tap Download for Android below (a signed .apk, about 3 MB).
  2. Open the file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then open Mint. It starts in Templates; tap Budget in the tab bar for your budgets. Web updates arrive automatically.
Download for Android

The Android build is on its way. Until then, open Mint in Chrome and tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen. It installs as an app with the same features.

Prefer budgets first? Install the web app from Chrome instead: it opens straight on your budget. The native share sheet works in both.

💻 Computer Free

  1. Open juw12.github.io/projects/mint in any modern browser.
  2. In Chrome or Edge, click Install in the address bar (or the button below) to get a desktop window.
  3. Your data stays on this device until you share a budget, and shared budgets are end-to-end encrypted.
