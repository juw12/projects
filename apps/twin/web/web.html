Personality test

10 questions score your risk tolerance (1–5) and investor-vs-trader style, pick one of four types and set your guard-rails (max loss per trade, daily stop, reward:risk).

Goal planner

10,000 simulated futures for your funds, monthly contributions and goal, built from real monthly total-return history (block bootstrap). Warns when history is too short to be honest.

📈
Paper trading

Fractional practice trades at real delayed prices, with a portfolio chart (Robinhood / Webull style).

🐐
Copy a famous investor

Reads a fund's latest SEC 13F (Buffett, Burry, Ackman…), shows what changed, copies it into your practice portfolio (eToro style, from public filings).

Day-trade checklist

Entry → exit → Time Machine (10,000 replays + history) → size → risk → ticket, powered by the open-source day-trader-skill.
