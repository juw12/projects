LUMBERYARD

Grow · Harvest · Sustain

Run a timber operation from seedling to sawmill. Click a mature tree to harvest it, click bare soil to plant a sapling. Keep the forest alive while you bank the profit.

Field Manual

  • Click a mature tree (full canopy) to harvest logs. It leaves bare soil.
  • Click bare soil to plant a sapling ($6). Trees regrow through 4 stages over time.
  • Forest % = share of plots with a living tree. Let it fall below 25% and the land suffers; at 0% the yard is barren and it's over.
  • Sell logs at market price, or hold them to fill orders for a fat bonus.
  • Upgrades: Sharpen Axe (more logs), Build Sawmill (better price), Irrigation (faster growth), Hire Crew (auto-harvest).
  • Crew costs wages each day. Miss payroll and they walk.
