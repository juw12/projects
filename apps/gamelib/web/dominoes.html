Dominoes

Classic draw/block dominoes with a double-six set (28 tiles). Each side draws 7 and matches either open end of the line.

Can't play? Draw from the boneyard until you can — or pass if it's empty. Round ends when a hand empties (opponent's pips are scored) or all players are blocked (lowest pips wins). Ties score nothing.
