📘 How to Play — Beginner Guide

1. The Goal

Clear the whole board by removing every tile. You remove tiles by matching them in identical pairs — click two tiles that show the same face and they disappear.

2. What is a "Free Tile"? (most important!)

You can only click a tile if it is free. A tile is free when BOTH are true:

  • Nothing is stacked on top of it — no tile covers it from a higher layer.
  • It is open on at least one horizontal side — the space immediately to its left OR right is empty.

If a tile has another tile pressing on it on both the left and the right, it is locked and cannot be picked.

3. How to Match

  • Click a free tile — it lifts up and glows gold.
  • Click a second free tile that looks identical.
  • Match! Both fade away and your move counter goes up.

Flowers (🌸) match any other flower. Seasons (🍂) match any other season.

5. Game Modes

  • Beginner — small 3-layer fan, easy, default.
  • Classic Turtle — the iconic 144-tile layout.
  • Pyramid — nested stacked square pyramid.
  • Timed — Turtle with a 10:00 countdown.
  • Connect — a different game: flat grid where you must find a clear path with at most 2 turns between matching tiles.

6. Controls

  • Hint — highlights a valid matching pair for a few seconds.
  • Shuffle — rearranges the tiles that remain (useful at a dead end).
  • Undo — puts back the pair you just removed.
  • Restart — replay the same layout. New Game — deal a fresh board.
