Painting: SAND

Rules of the sandbox

  • Sand falls and piles up; it sinks through water and oil.
  • Water falls and flows to find its level; it douses fire into smoke.
  • Stone is solid and static — build walls and floors with it.
  • Wood is static but burns; fire spreads into it and makes smoke.
  • Fire rises, spreads to flammables (wood, oil, plant) and emits smoke.
  • Oil is a flammable liquid — it floats on water and ignites fast.
  • Smoke rises and drifts, then fades away.
  • Plant grows by spreading into touching water; it is very flammable.
  • Lava flows slowly, ignites flammables, melts stone, and water turns it to stone.
  • Eraser removes any material under the brush.
  • Keys: P pause · C clear · 1-9 pick · E eraser · [ ] brush size
