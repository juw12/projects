Mahjong Solitaire

Clear the turtle-shaped stack by matching free pairs of identical tiles.

A tile is free when nothing rests on top of it and either its left or right side is open. Tap two free tiles with the same shape & color to remove them. Clear every tile to win. Use Hint to reveal a pair and Shuffle (or an automatic shuffle) when you get stuck. Boards are always generated solvable. Best (lowest) time is saved per difficulty.
