🧋 Bubble Tea

Run your own bubble-tea stall

Customers order a drink: a tea base, a milk level, a sugar level and their toppings. Tap the ingredients below to build each cup, then hit SERVE. Match the order exactly for a perfect bonus and extra reputation. Let a customer's patience run out and your reputation drops — at zero the shop closes for good.

How to Play

1. Read the ticket on the front customer card.
2. Tap a Tea, a Milk level and a Sugar level — each is required.
3. Tap toppings to add/remove them (limited by your cup slots).
4. Press SERVE to hand over the cup.
5. Exact match = PERFECT: bonus coins + reputation.
6. Between days, spend coins on upgrades to unlock new teas, toppings and comforts.

Keyboard: Space serve · Backspace empty cup.
