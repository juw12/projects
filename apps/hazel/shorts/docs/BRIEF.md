# Hazel Shorts (formerly Shortcraft) — brief

## Why

Faceless explainer accounts ("what's inside", "how it's made", "why does…") grow fast with few posts —
reference accounts reached 40K+ followers within roughly a dozen posts (public profile counts, Oct 2026).
Bahasa Indonesia versions of the format are still comparatively thin. Making these videos normally needs
After Effects/CapCut skills and stock assets; Hazel Shorts turns the format into a fill-in-the-scenes tool.

## Who

Engineers, teachers and hobbyists with real knowledge (e.g. NPI/DVT, product teardown, physics) who want
to publish short explainers without showing their face or learning motion design.

## Format rules baked in

1. Hook in the first scene (question or surprising claim), ≤ ~6 s.
2. One idea per scene, one concrete number where possible.
3. Burned-in captions in short chunks; numbers highlighted.
4. Close with a question or "what next?" to drive comments.
5. Keep it ≤ 60 s for Shorts; post EN and ID versions and compare retention.

## Monetization notes (verified Oct 2026)

- TikTok Creator Rewards is **not** available in Indonesia.
- YouTube Partner Program is available in Indonesia: 1K subscribers + 10M Shorts views in 90 days
  (ad revenue share), or 500 subscribers + 3M Shorts views for the lower fan-funding tier.

## Origin

Built from a hand-rendered pilot: four videos (CCTV teardown and NPI "5 gates", each in EN and ID)
rendered with the Python pipeline in `pipeline/examples/`, then generalized into the browser studio.
