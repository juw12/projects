Two proven formats

Editorial step-tracker (white, serif, numbered steps) and dark teardown with callout cards — the styles behind fast-growing faceless explainer accounts.

Your voice, natural

Record each scene with your mic or upload clips. Scene timing follows the audio. Browser voice is available for quick timing previews.

Captions + music built in

Short, punchy caption chunks with number highlights, plus soft generated music — no stock licenses to worry about.

Viral checklist

Checks hook length, total ≤ 60 s, a concrete number and a closing question — the basics that keep people watching and commenting.

Post kit

Generates a title, caption and hashtags (EN or ID) from your scenes, ready to paste.

Channel Kit + publishing

Turn one prompt into channel names, bios, a 30-day plan, avatar and banner. Upload straight to YouTube, share to TikTok / Instagram from your phone, or schedule everything with the autopost pipeline.

Private and offline-first

No account, no server: projects live in your browser and export as JSON. Works on GitHub Pages.

How it works

  1. Open the studio and load a sample, or start blank.
  2. Edit each scene: headline, big number, emoji or image, and the narration.
  3. Record your voice per scene (or upload clips), then press Play to check timing.
  4. Export the video and copy the post kit. Post the same idea in EN and ID and compare retention.

Want studio-grade neural voices or full-quality offline renders? See the optional Python pipeline in the project's pipeline/ folder.
