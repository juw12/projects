Hazel Shortsformerly Shortcraft

Faceless 9:16 explainer shorts from a few scenes.

  • Hook → steps → outro, four templates (Editorial, Teardown, Pop, Clean)
  • Your voice or browser voice, auto captions, generated music
  • 1080×1920 MP4 export, post kit, YouTube upload, Channel Kit

Hazel Spedformerly Sped

Sped-up, slowed and mashup edits of your own songs.

  • Describe it (“faster, higher, 20 seconds”) or tune tempo and pitch
  • Live 9:16 preview with text, themes and a beat visualizer
  • MP4 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, plus WAV and cover image

Hazel Longthe original Hazel

Long-form videos: create, edit, repurpose and publish.

  • Generate a storyboard and preview from a prompt, then refine it
  • Publish packages for every platform, plus a media library
  • Desktop app: auto-cut silences and filler words with ffmpeg + Whisper
