Welcome

AI for the 9-to-5. Tested at work, so you don't have to.

Desk Verdict is a Verdict Scorecard for AI tools at work. Test one tool on one real task, score it, and get a verdict you can say out loud in 30 seconds.

How It Works
1
Score a tool

Rate the five criteria on the 1 to 5 scorecard.

2
Get the verdict

A live USE IT / WAIT / SKIP badge updates as you rate.

3
Save it

Every verdict is stored in your database.

4
Say it in 30 seconds

The app writes the verdict script for you.

5
Browse the database

Search and filter to find which AI for which job.

Verdict Scale
USE IT
Saves real time, works reliably, safe for normal work use.
WAIT
Promising but unreliable, expensive, or missing a key feature. Revisit in 1-3 months.
SKIP
Hype, poor results, or not worth the learning curve. Work-safety of 2 or lower is always a SKIP.
