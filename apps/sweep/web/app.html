Why install Sweep?

  • Guided intake — Type your idea in plain words. Sweep drafts the brief locally — no AI, no network
  • Build → Grow → Earn plans — Complete roadmap from spec to launch to revenue, all generated locally
  • Works offline — Your data is encrypted at rest in your browser. No server, no network required
  • Full-screen experience — Clean, distraction-free interface focused on your work
