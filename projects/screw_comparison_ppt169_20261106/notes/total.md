# Screw Source Comparison — Speaker Notes

## 01_cover

Title slide. Introduce the topic: comparing main and second source screws from BOM.

## 02_overview

Both screws are mechanically identical — same thread (M1.7), length (4.5mm), head (K), and drive (Torx T6). The key distinction is the "-S-" prefix in the part number, which marks the second source in PEGATRON's PLM naming convention. HARMONY is the primary supplier; SCREWTECH is the approved alternate.

## 03_drawing

2D technical drawing of the screw. Left: side profile showing head (K-type), threaded shaft (M1.7 tapping), and tip with 4.5mm overall length dimension. Right: head top view showing the Torx T6 6-lobe internal drive pattern. Bottom: coating swatches comparing White Zinc (main) vs Blue Zinc (second source).

## 04_comparison

Walk through the spec table row by row. Six attributes are identical (thread, length, head, drive, thread type, spec). Two differ: coating (White Zinc vs Blue Zinc) and supplier. Emphasize that the mechanical fit is unaffected — the differences are surface treatment and sourcing.

## 05_differences

Three differences: (1) Coating — Blue Zinc has better corrosion resistance and is often used for cosmetic or specific environmental requirements; (2) Supplier — HARMONY vs SCREWTECH, dual-sourcing for supply continuity; (3) Part number encoding — QU vs QN reflects the plating variant, and the -S- prefix marks it as second source. None of these affect mechanical fit.

## 06_bottom_line

The two screws are mechanically interchangeable. However, substitution from main to second source requires verifying that the product spec does not mandate a specific coating type or color. If the spec is silent on plating, the second source is a direct substitute. Next steps: review the product drawing, then confirm both sources in the BOM if clear.
