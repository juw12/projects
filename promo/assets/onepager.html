nudge 💌

See which friends you've been leaving on read while you doomscroll.

The problem

Friends send you reels and links. You scroll past them. The apps track your attention to the second, but none of them tell you who's waiting on a reply. A shared reel is a small "I thought of you," and it disappears under the same feed it came from.

How it decides

A reply within 48h counts as answering a share. Each friend gets a priority score from recency, unanswered streaks, who reaches out after silence, and the ratio of what they've shared to what you've returned.

What it does

  • Reads the chat export you already downloaded (Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, iMessage).
  • Shows who you've left on read and which specific shares you never answered.
  • Gives you a ready-to-send opener to reconnect.
  • Weekly digest + desktop notification, and an iOS widget that nudges you before you open Instagram.
  • Ships as a Python CLI and an installable browser app (PWA).
Private by design. 100% local. No network calls, no accounts, no telemetry, pure Python standard library. It reads only exports you download from your own accounts, with no scraping and no unofficial APIs. Nothing leaves your device.
