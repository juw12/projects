# Respark - Sales Playbook

Everything you need to start conversations and close deals. No theory. Copy-paste the templates; adapt the bracketed parts.

---

## 0. The offer in one sentence

> "We help local businesses bring back past customers with automated, personalized email + SMS - usually 8-15% reactivation rate within 30 days. $497 setup, $97/mo."

That's it. Say this in your first 10 seconds.

---

## 1. Who to target (in order of priority)

Best prospects = businesses with these signals:

- 10+ Google reviews (proven business, has customers to reactivate)
- Website is old, slow, or generic (signals owner isn't tech-savvy)
- Owner-operated (decision maker is reachable)
- No active email/SMS marketing visible (gap we fill)
- Has a booking system, POS, or CRM (we can pull data from)

**Top 5 industries to start with** (highest close rate, simplest data):
1. Plumbers / HVAC
2. Dentists
3. Hair salons / spas
4. Personal trainers / boutique gyms
5. Auto mechanics

Skip restaurants for now - they have high churn and lower close rates.

---

## 2. How to find prospects (free methods)

You don't need a scraper. Use these:

**Method A - Google Maps (fastest)**
1. Go to google.com/maps
2. Search "[industry] in [your city]"
3. Open each listing. Note: business name, owner name (if visible), phone, website URL, review count
4. Add to prospects.csv

**Method B - Yelp**
1. Search "[industry] [your city]"
2. Filter by "Highest rated" - these are the businesses most likely to have money and care about their reputation

**Method C - Facebook/Instagram**
1. Search "[industry] [your city]"
2. Look at local businesses with active pages but no obvious email automation
3. Owner is often in the bio - that's your pitch target

**Target number:** Find 50 prospects before you send a single email. Quality > quantity, but volume matters too.

[your phone]

### Sending rules

- Send between 7am-9am in the prospect's local time
- 1 email per prospect. Follow up only if they reply.
- Personalize the [bracketed bits]. Generic emails get ignored.
- Don't attach anything. Don't link to a calendar. Just ask for a reply.
- Use a real email address (gmail works fine for starting out). Don't use yourname@gmail.com - use something like juw.reactivation@gmail.com (set up a free Google account).

## 5. The 15-minute sales call

Once they reply, book a 15-min call (Zoom or phone - phone is easier for older business owners).

### Opening (30 seconds)

> "Hey [first name], thanks for taking the time. I'll be quick. Two questions: what are you doing right now to bring back past customers, and roughly how many past customers do you have in your system?"

Listen. Don't pitch yet.

### Discovery (3-5 minutes)

Ask these questions, in order:
1. "How many past customers do you have that haven't booked in 6+ months?"
2. "What happens when someone falls off - do you have a system to re-engage them?"
3. "What does a reactivated customer typically spend with you?" (i.e. what's the LTV?)
4. "Are you doing any email or SMS marketing right now?"

Their answers reveal the gap. Don't tell them. Let them see it.

### Pitch (3 minutes)

> "Here's what I do: I set up an automated system that sends past customers a personalized 5-message campaign over 30 days - mix of email and SMS. Average client sees 8-15% re-engagement, so if you have 500 past customers, that's 40-75 reactivated bookings.

> Setup is $497 - that's the work to pull your data, write the campaign, and launch it. Then $97/mo covers ongoing management and reporting.

> Want me to show you a sample campaign for [business name] right now?"

Pull up demo. Click their industry. Walk through 1-2 messages. **Show, don't tell.**

### Close (3 minutes)

> "Two ways to start: I can do a free trial for 30 days - if you don't see results, you pay nothing. Or if you're ready, we kick off this week. Which sounds better?"

If they hesitate:

> "What's the hesitation? Is it the price, or is it just that you want to think about it?"

If price: "If I can show you 10 reactivated customers in the first month, this pays for itself in one job, right?"

If thinking: "Totally fair. Want me to send you the sample campaign and you can take a look this week?"

### After the call

- Send a follow-up email within 1 hour with: the demo link, a one-paragraph summary of what you discussed, and a clear next step ("I'll send over the proposal tomorrow morning")
- If they said "send me info," send the demo link, then follow up in 3 days
- If they said yes: send the contract, ask for the customer list, schedule a kickoff call

## 6. Objection handlers

**"I'm not sure this works for my industry."**
> "Fair - every industry is a little different. Let me show you a sample for [their industry] right now." [Pull up demo.html]

**"I don't have time for this."**
> "That's exactly the point - after setup, you do nothing. The system runs itself. The 30 minutes we spend this week saves you hundreds of hours over the next year."

**"How do I know it'll work?"**
> "Honest answer: I can't guarantee numbers, but my average is 8-15% re-engagement. If we run it for 30 days and you see less than 5%, I'll refund the setup fee. Want to try it that way?"

**"I'm not technical."**
> "Perfect - neither are most of my clients. I handle everything. You just need to give me access to your customer list."

**"Can I just do this myself with Mailchimp?"**
> "Sure, in theory. But the difference is I write the campaign, set up the timing, handle the SMS, and you get a monthly report showing ROI. Most owners don't actually do it when they're busy running the business."

**"Send me some info."**
> "Done - I'll send the demo link right now. When's a good time to follow up - Thursday or Friday?"

## 7. Pricing framework

| Tier | What you offer | Price |
|------|----------------|-------|
| Starter | Setup + 1 campaign, 1 month support | $497 once |
| Growth | Setup + unlimited campaigns + monthly report | $497 + $97/mo |
| Premium | Setup + unlimited + dedicated Slack + quarterly reviews | $497 + $297/mo |

**Always start with Growth.** It's the easiest upsell once they're seeing results.

If they push back on price, say:
> "If this brings back even 5 customers in the first month, what's each of those customers worth to you? [Wait for answer.] So one month of this pays for itself many times over."

If they say yes but can't afford $497 upfront: offer to split into 2 payments ($250 + $247). Don't go lower - that signals you don't believe in your own service.

## 8. What to do AFTER you close

1. Ask for their customer list (CSV export from their system)
2. Schedule a 30-min kickoff call to confirm voice/tone
3. Build the campaign (customize the template for their business)
4. Send 2 sample messages for approval
5. Launch the campaign
6. Report weekly for the first month

For steps 1-6, you'll work with me. Send me the customer list (or describe the business) and I'll build the campaign for you. You handle the client relationship.

---

## 9. Mindset

- **You're not selling software. You're selling "more revenue with less work."** Every word should connect to that.
- **80% of your prospects will say no.** That's normal. Keep going.
- **Your job is to get 3 conversations.** Not to close on the first call.
- **The first 3 clients are the hardest.** After that, you'll have case studies and referrals.
