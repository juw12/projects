**Category:** SaaS product marketing
**Direction:** EN → ZH (Simplified Chinese)

### Source (English)

Every great team starts with one problem: too many conversations scattered across five different tools, with no single source of truth.

Flowline brings your team's work into a single, calm workspace. No more alt-tabbing between Slack, Notion, and Jira. No more "did anyone see that?" threads that die in a direct message. No more Monday morning scramble to rebuild context before standup. Just one place where tasks live, decisions get made, and knowledge actually sticks.

Built for distributed teams of 5 to 200, Flowline adapts to how you already work. Import your projects in minutes, not weeks. Set up custom workflows without touching a line of code. Assign follow-ups directly from a comment, and they appear on the right person's board before they even ask.

And when the 3 PM sync ends, your team knows exactly who's doing what, because Flowline keeps everyone on the same page, literally.

Start free. No credit card required. No 47-step onboarding wizard. Just open the app, invite your team, and get roughly 20% of your week back.

### Translation (Simplified Chinese)

每个高效团队的起点，都是同一个问题：对话散落在五个不同的工具里，没有统一的信息源。

Flowline 把团队的工作收拢进一个安静、专注的工作空间。不用再在 Slack、Notion 和 Jira 之间来回切换，不用再把"有人看到这条消息吗？"发出去然后石沉大海，也不用在周一站会前手忙脚乱地重建上下文。所有任务、决策和知识，都在同一个地方沉淀下来。

Flowline 专为 5 到 200 人的远程团队设计，适应你们现有的工作方式。几分钟内完成项目导入，无需编写代码即可搭建自定义工作流。在评论区直接指派后续任务，它会自动出现在对应成员的看板上，不用等对方来问。

3 点的同步会结束后，团队每个人都清楚谁在做什么、下一步是什么，因为 Flowline 让大家始终在同一页上，字面意义上的同一页。

免费开始，无需信用卡，没有 47 步的引导流程。打开应用，邀请团队，把每周约 20% 的时间还给自己。

### Context

Source is a fictional SaaS landing page for a project management tool targeting startup founders and ops managers (teams of 5–200, remote-first). The Chinese translation preserves the casual, direct tone of the English while shifting sentence structure to match natural Simplified Chinese marketing cadence (shorter clauses, verb-final rhythm). Brand name "Flowline" is kept untranslated per standard practice for tech brands entering the Chinese market.

## Sample 2 — Chinese to English (Simplified)

**Category:** Premium brand storytelling
**Direction:** ZH (Simplified Chinese) → EN
*Original composition mimicking a WeChat Official Account brand story.*

### Source (Simplified Chinese)

清晨六点，武夷山的雾气还没有散尽。

我们的制茶师傅老陈，已经坐在岩壁下的焙房前，开始了一天的工作。他是这片山第四代做茶的人，手上有一道被炭火烫出的疤，他从不觉得那是伤，他说那是山给他的标记。

我们的"岩韵·正岩肉桂"，只用武夷核心产区海拔六百米以上的正岩茶青。每年秋茶季，老陈带团队徒步进山，按"看天做茶"的老规矩，根据当天的天气和叶片的状态决定萎凋的时间。不赶产量，不拼速度。一炉茶从采摘到成品，至少经过七十二道工序，耗时超过四十天。

我们相信，好茶不该被包装成奢侈品。所以这一款肉桂，定价只取行业均价的七成。让你不用等节日，不用送人，只是某个安静的周末下午，给自己泡一杯，闻一闻桂皮与山岩混在一起的气息，知道它来自一座具体的山、一双手、一段具体的时间。

### Translation (English)

At six in the morning, the mist over Wuyi Mountain has not yet lifted.

Our master tea maker, Old Chen, is already at the roasting room beneath the rock face, beginning the day's work. He is the fourth generation of his family to make tea in these hills. There is a burn scar on the back of his hand from charcoal fires, and he does not call it a wound. He calls it a mark the mountain left on him.

Our "Yanyun · Core Rock Cinnamon Oolong" is made exclusively from fresh leaves grown above 600 meters in the core Wuyi rock gardens. Every autumn harvest, Old Chen leads the team up the trail on foot, following the old rule of "making tea by the sky," adjusting the withering time based on that day's weather and the state of the leaves. No rushing volume. No chasing speed. A single batch moves through at least seventy-two processes from picking to finished tea, taking more than forty days.

We believe great tea should not be packaged as a luxury. That is why this cinnamon oolong is priced at seventy percent of the industry average. So you do not have to wait for a holiday or a gift-giving occasion. Just a quiet weekend afternoon, a cup for yourself, the scent of cassia bark and stone mixing in the air, and the knowledge that it came from a specific mountain, a pair of hands, and a stretch of time you can actually name.

### Context

Source is a WeChat Official Account style brand story for a premium Chinese rock oolong (岩茶) producer, written in the literary-but-approachable register typical of DTC tea brands like 八马 or 华祥苑. The English translation preserves the sensory, slow-paced cadence and the brand's anti-luxury positioning while adapting cultural references (正岩, 看天做茶, 岩韵) into natural English that a Western specialty tea buyer would understand without footnotes.

## Sample 3 — Product Description Rewrite (Before / After)

**Category:** Kitchen & Dining (insulated water bottle)
**Direction:** EN → EN (copywriting rewrite)

### BEFORE

316 Stainless Steel Vacuum Insulated Water Bottle, 24oz/710ml Sports Water Bottle with Straw Lid, BPA Free Thermos for Gym, Outdoor, Travel, Leak Proof Double Wall Coffee Cup, 8 Color Options

Feature:
1. Made of 316 stainless steel, food grade material, safe for your health.
2. Double wall vacuum insulation, keep drinks cold 24 hours, hot 12 hours.
3. Comes with straw lid and sip lid, 2 lids in one bottle.
4. Leak proof design, no worry about water leaking in your bag.
5. BPA free, no plastic taste, easy to clean.
6. Available in 8 colors, suitable for men and women.

Specification:
Material: 316 Stainless Steel
Capacity: 24oz/710ml
Size: 27cm x 7cm
Color: 8 Colors

Package List:
1 x Water Bottle
1 x Straw Lid
1 x Sip Lid

### AFTER

Your 7 AM iced coffee is still cold at 4 PM. Your 8 AM hot tea is still warm at lunch. That is not a feature. That is just how a bottle should work.

The 710ml Peak Bottle uses 316 stainless steel (the same grade found in surgical instruments) with a double-wall vacuum chamber that holds temperature for 24 hours cold, 12 hours hot. No condensation. No sweating. No wet bag.

Two lids included: a silicone straw lid for the car and desk, and a wide-mouth sip lid for the trail. Both are genuinely leak-proof, not "mostly leak-proof."

The interior is powder-coated, so no metal taste, no plastic smell. Dishwasher safe, and the wide mouth fits an ice cube with room to spare.

Eight colors. One bottle you will actually reach for every morning. Order today, and it is at your door before your current one finally gives out.

### Context

The "before" version is a typical keyword-stuffed, bullet-point Amazon listing produced by a non-native English seller: functional but flat, no emotional hook, no narrative arc, no CTA. The "after" version keeps every factual claim identical (316 steel, 24h/12h, two lids, leak-proof, BPA-free, 8 colors) but restructures around a concrete morning scenario, adds sensory and social-proof language ("surgical instruments," "the bottle you're about to return"), and closes with a direct, low-friction CTA. The quality jump is measurable: the rewrite moves from zero emotional engagement and zero CTA to a scenario-driven narrative with a closing call to action, which is the structural difference between a listing that gets scrolled past and one that gets clicked.
