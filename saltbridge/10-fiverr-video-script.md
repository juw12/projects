# Fiverr Gig Video Script — 30 seconds

Fiverr gigs with a video get **significantly more impressions and clicks** than text-only gigs. You don't need production quality — a phone recording in good light works fine. Record this on your phone, upload to Fiverr as your gig video.

**Setup:**
- Phone held horizontally (landscape)
- Natural light from a window behind the camera (lights your face)
- Plain background (wall, bookshelf, plants — not your actual bedroom)
- Phone propped on something stable (no shaky cam)
- 1080p or higher if your phone supports it
- Quiet room (no echo, no traffic noise)

---

## Shot-by-shot script

Total runtime: **30 seconds**. Don't try to fit more — short and clear beats long and packed.

### Shot 1 — Hook (0-5s)
**Camera:** Medium close-up on your face, slight smile.
**You say:** "Hi, I'm a bilingual EN / 中文 writer."

### Shot 2 — What you do (5-15s)
**Camera:** Same position. Hold up your phone or a piece of paper showing the words.
**You say:** "I translate English to Chinese and Chinese to English — for business, marketing, and product content."
**B-roll option:** Cut to a screen recording of you highlighting text in a sample document.

### Shot 3 — Proof (15-22s)
**Camera:** Tilt down to show your laptop screen or a printed portfolio sample.
**You say:** "Recent work includes a SaaS marketing site translation, an Amazon catalog rewrite, and bilingual email sequences."
**Visual:** Show 2-3 portfolio samples on screen (the 3 from `03-portfolio-samples.md`).

### Shot 4 — Why you (22-27s)
**Camera:** Back to face, slight lean-in.
**You say:** "Native both sides, 24-hour turnaround, per-client glossary on every project."

### Shot 5 — CTA (27-30s)
**Camera:** Same position, friendly expression.
**You say:** "Message me with a sample — I'll send back a free preview. Looking forward to working with you."

---

## Upload tips

- File format: MP4
- Max size: 100 MB (Fiverr's limit — most phone recordings are well under)
- Title your file: `gig-1-translation-video.mp4` (so you know which gig it's for)
- Upload to Fiverr as "Gallery → Video" — you can upload one per gig
- If you don't want to appear on camera, do a screen-recording version: open `01-fiverr-gigs.md`, scroll through the gig text slowly, narrate the script over it

---

## No-camera alternative (screen recording only, 30 seconds)

If you're not ready to be on camera, this works too:

**Slide 1 (0-5s):** Black screen + white text: "Bilingual EN/中文 · Native both sides"
**Slide 2 (5-15s):** Open your Canva gig image for Gig 1 + voiceover the "what you do" lines
**Slide 3 (15-22s):** Screen recording of one portfolio sample (translate a few lines live, show before/after)
**Slide 4 (22-30s):** Same Canva image + voiceover CTA

Record with PowerPoint's "Record Slide Show" feature, or OBS (free), or your phone's screen recorder.

---

## When to record

**Don't record today.** Record on Day 2-3 of your launch, after you've posted the 3 gigs. That way:
- You're not blocked on getting video before launching
- You can reference the actual live gig URLs in the video (more credibility)
- You can do a re-take once you've settled into the work

**Day 7 optimization:** After you have 3-5 orders, if your impressions are still low, that's the time to add the video and see if it moves the needle.
