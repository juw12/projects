## Insights for your launch

1. **Don't fight on reviews.** Vivien Lee has 5,790. You have 0. Trying to outrank her in "chinese translation" general search is a 12-month losing battle. Instead, target long-tail searches where the top sellers don't rank:
   - "chinese translation business copywriting"
   - "amazon listing chinese seller english"
   - "bilingual email newsletter english chinese"
   - "saas localization chinese"

2. **Lead with Gig 2 (product descriptions) in your outbound messaging.** It's the gap with the clearest, most monetizable wedge. Translation is your volume/review engine. Email is the premium retainer play.

3. **Your pricing is correctly positioned across all 3 gigs.** No changes needed.

4. **The 1-hour response time is table stakes.** Vivien Lee, Chen S, Bangyou Xiang all average 1 hour. If you're slower than 1 hour, your ranking drops. Set up notifications.

5. **Top Rated badge is your 6-month goal.** Requirements: 100+ orders, $20k+ earnings, 4.7+ rating, 90%+ response rate, 90%+ on-time delivery, <5% cancellation. With the math from `research/track-1-research.md`, this is achievable by month 6 if you execute the daily routine.

6. **Consider adding Fiverr Pro application to your 90-day roadmap.** Once you hit Level 1 with strong metrics, you can apply. Pro tier commands 3-5x the rates you're pricing at today. Christian Pang and Bangyou Xiang are Pro sellers — they're living proof of the upside.
