# 02 — Cold Outreach: Week 1 Pack (Chinese E-Commerce Sellers)

Five ready-to-customize cold outreach templates targeting Chinese cross-border e-commerce sellers. Each targets a different channel and seller type. The **free 150-word rewrite sample** is the entire conversion mechanism — it's in every template.

Replace `[bracketed fields]` per target. Pricing is reference; adjust per the research in `research/track-1-research.md`.

---

## Template 1 — Amazon Buyer-Seller Message (single-product rewrite)

**Target:** A Chinese-brand Amazon seller with one hero product that has weak English copy. Great photos, translated-feel title and bullets.

**Channel:** Amazon Buyer-Seller Message (send after buying their product or asking a question — Amazon gates you out otherwise).

```
Hi [brand name] team — quick observation. Your [product] listing reads like a direct translation, which makes English-speaking buyers hesitate before they click. I can see you've put real effort into the photos and the product itself; the listing just isn't doing it justice.

I rewrote the first 150 words as a free sample — same product, same features, written the way US/UK buyers respond to. Want me to send it over?

No cost, no catch.
```

**Why it works:** Names a specific observation (not generic praise), acknowledges the effort already made (so it doesn't read as criticism), offers the sample (the conversion mechanism), low-pressure ask. ~120 words.

## Template 2 — Cold email (full-catalog rewrite pitch)

**Target:** A Chinese Shopify seller running $20-50K/mo with 50-200 SKUs. English site but translation-feel copy. Find via their "About" or "Contact" page.

**Channel:** Email

**Subject:** Your [product category] listings are leaving conversions on the table

```
Hi [first name],

I came across your Shopify store and spent a few minutes reading through [product category] listings. The English copy is doing the product photography a disservice — buyers land on a beautiful page and then read text that reads translated, not written for them.

Quick context on me: I'm bilingual EN/中文, write native-grade English for Chinese e-commerce sellers, and I keep per-client glossaries so a 50-SKU catalog stays consistent across future updates.

I rewrote the first 150 words of one of your [product] listings as a free sample. If you like it, I'd love to talk about cleaning up the full catalog or starting with your top 10 sellers. If not, you keep a free upgrade either way.

Want me to send the sample?

— Juw
Bilingual EN / 中文 · [your URL or LinkedIn]
```

**Why it works:** Specific observation (translated-feel, not generic), positions bilingual as a real differentiator with concrete proof (glossary consistency), free sample, low-pressure framing. ~150 words.

## Template 3 — Instagram DM (factory-direct seller)

**Target:** A Chinese factory's Instagram account (@[brand].official or @factory.[city]) advertising products directly, with weak English captions.

**Channel:** Instagram DM

```
Hey [handle] — saw your [product] posts, looks great.

Quick question: are you happy with how your English captions convert when DMs come in? I write English product copy for Chinese sellers and have a free 150-word rewrite I could send over to show what's possible when the English actually reads native.

Worth a try?
```

**Why it works:** Specific (mentions their posts), opens with a question (DM etiquette — questions get more replies than statements), free sample, casual tone matching IG. ~70 words.

## Template 4 — WeChat (after finding handle via packaging)

**Target:** A Chinese Etsy seller whose packaging includes a WeChat QR. Likely has decent English on Etsy but a Chinese-only backend for ops.

**Channel:** WeChat 1:1 message

```
[Brand name]您好 — 在 Etsy 上看到你们的 [product]，包装很用心。

英文 listing 写得不错，但 Etsy 后台和客服回复如果也是中英混着，海外买家有时候会犹豫。我做英文 listing 和客服回复的优化，写得跟 native English 一样。如果有需要可以先发 150 字 sample 给你看。

方便的话我发个 sample 给你？
```

**Why it works:** Opens in 中文 (matches their language), specific observation (packaging detail shows you've actually looked), frames the bilingual value as helping THEM (海外买家 conversion), free sample, soft close. ~110 Chinese characters.

## Template 5 — LinkedIn message (founder of cross-border brand)

**Channel:** LinkedIn message

```

I work with cross-border e-commerce founders on EN listing optimization — bilingual EN/中文, native-grade English that reads the way US/UK buyers respond to. Recent example: [a Shenzhen-based home goods seller saw a 35% lift in conversion on rewritten listings / placeholder for your real one].

I'd love to send a 150-word sample rewrite of one of your listings — no cost, no commitment. Just message me which product you want me to start with.

— Juw
Bilingual EN / 中文 · [your site or contact]
```

**Why it works:** Opens with a compliment that shows real engagement (photography + pricing — not generic), names the actual conversion lever, positions bilingual as a defensible edge, names a specific outcome (35% lift), free sample with a clear ask. ~150 words.

## Customization checklist (before you send any of these)

- [ ] **Name a SPECIFIC observation.** "I noticed your [exact product] has [exact weak spot in copy]" beats "I love your store" every time.
- [ ] Replace all `[bracketed fields]` with the real target's details (do NOT bulk-send the same template)
- [ ] For Amazon messages: actually buy or message the seller first to unlock the Buyer-Seller channel
- [ ] For WeChat: actually find their QR (don't fake it — sellers verify who scanned)
- [ ] For LinkedIn: check the founder's recent posts for something you can reference (comment-on-a-post-then-DM works better than cold DM)
- [ ] **Never send without a free sample offer.** The sample IS the pitch.
- [ ] If they reply: respond in their language (中文 if they wrote 中文, English if English)
