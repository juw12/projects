# 04 — Cold Outreach: Chinese E-Commerce Sellers (Bilingual Specialization)

This is the niche-specialized cold outreach for the bilingual freelance track — targets Chinese cross-border e-commerce sellers on Amazon, Etsy, Shopify, and via email/IG/WeChat.

**For the general "AI content for local businesses" version** (Instagram DMs to restaurants/salons/gyms, Bangkok/ASEAN SMB focus), see:
- `../side-hustle-playbook/2-cold-dm-script.md` — general framework for IG-first local biz outreach

This version is deliberately different: **higher-ticket projects, different channels, cross-border pain.** Do not treat them as interchangeable.

---

## Target profile

Chinese Amazon / Etsy / Shopify sellers with weak English listings. Look for:

- Product titles that read like direct translation (broken word order, wrong register)
- Descriptions with Chinglish, missing benefit framing, no sensory language
- Listing photos are great but copy doesn't match the visual quality
- Owner responds in 中文 when you message in English
- Revenue range: **$5K–$100K/month** — big enough to afford a copywriter, small enough to actually reply

Skip these: sellers whose English is already strong (they have someone), sub-$5K/month sellers (can't afford it), pure resellers/dropshippers with no brand to protect (won't pay for quality).

## Where to find them

| Channel | How to find |
|---|---|
| Amazon | Search your niche category, filter for Chinese-brand sellers, scan listings for translated-feel copy |
| Etsy | Same approach — many dropshippers with translated descriptions |
| Alibaba | Look up factories → check their Amazon/Shopify stores |
| Instagram | Search factory-direct accounts in your niche, DM |
| LinkedIn | Search "Amazon seller" + "Shenzhen / Yiwu / Guangzhou / 义乌 / 深圳" |
| Facebook groups | "Amazon FBA", "Etsy sellers" — many Chinese sellers lurking |
| WeChat | Find brand WeChat IDs on their packaging or website |

## The offer: free sample first

**Don't pitch a service. Pitch a free 150-word rewrite of one of their existing listings.** This does three things:

1. Proves you can write native-grade English
2. Forces them to see the before/after gap
3. Makes the follow-up sale almost trivial

The sample is your entire sales pitch. The product is the work itself.

## Scripts

### Amazon Buyer-Seller Message
*(Send after buying their product or asking a question — Amazon gates you out otherwise.)*

```
Hi [brand name] team — quick observation. Your [product] listing reads like a direct translation, which makes English-speaking buyers hesitate before they even click.

I rewrote the first 150 words as a free sample — same product, same features, written the way US/UK buyers respond to. Want me to send it over?

No cost, no catch.
```

### Cold email

```
Subject: Your [product category] listing is leaving conversions on the table

Hi [first name],

I came across your [Amazon/Etsy/Shopify] listing for [product name] and the English copy isn't doing the product justice — it's clearly translated, not written for an English-speaking buyer.

I rewrote the first 150 words as a free sample. Same product, same facts, just written the way buyers in [US/UK] actually respond to.

If you like it, I'd love to talk about cleaning up your full catalog. If not, you keep a free upgrade either way.

Want me to send the sample?

— Juw
Bilingual EN / 中文 · Amazon listing specialist
```

### Instagram DM

```
Hey [handle] — saw your [product] ad, looks great.

Quick question: are you happy with how your English listing converts? I rewrite Amazon/Etsy descriptions for Chinese sellers and have a free 150-word sample I can send to show what's possible.

Useful?
```

### WeChat (bilingual advantage — most local-business scripts don't have this)

```
[Brand name]您好 — 在 [Amazon/Etsy] 上看到你们的 [product]。

英文文案目前是翻译腔，欧美买家看到会犹豫。我重写了产品的前 150 字，免费发给你看看 — 如果觉得有用，再聊合作。
```

## Follow-up cadence

| Day | Action | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Initial outreach | (template above) |
| 3 | Bump #1 | "Did the sample land? Happy to redo it on a different product if you'd prefer." |
| 7 | Final bump | "Closing the loop — won't reach out again. If timing's not right, no worries. The free sample's yours either way." |
| 14+ | Move on | Do NOT burn the contact. They may come back in 3 months. |

## Tracking sheet

Open a Google Sheet. Columns:

`brand | contact_channel | handle | product_url | date_sent | response_status | followup_date | notes`

Aim for **10 outreach messages per day = 70/week**. At 1-2h/day side gig, this fits.

---

## Realistic conversion math

- Cold reply rate: **1-3%** (10-30 replies per week if you send 100/week)
- Reply → free sample accepted: ~50%
- Sample → paying customer: **20-30%**
- **Result**: 100 messages → 5 sample accepts → 1-2 paying customers

First project size: **$50-300** per catalog rewrite. Repeat clients move to monthly retainers ($200-500/mo).

The math is honest: higher effort than Fiverr, **higher margin when it works**. Use Fiverr for fast first-dollar, use cold outreach for compounding retainer revenue.

---

## What makes this work (and what kills it)

**Works when:**
- Sample is genuinely well-written, not a 5-minute job
- You rewrite THEIR product, not generic template text
- You email/DM in 中文 when they reply in 中文 (signal you actually understand them)
- Follow-ups are spaced and polite, not aggressive

**Kills it when:**
- You pitch without a sample (looks like every other spam)
- Sample is mediocre (no second chance)
- You badger past day 7 (they block, and you'll never recover)
- You mass-send the same sample to everyone (it's not customized, they can tell)
