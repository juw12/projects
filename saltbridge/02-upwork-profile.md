### Profile Overview

Your Chinese-speaking customers are ignoring your English copy. Or your English market can't parse your Mandarin marketing. I fix that gap. I translate and write bilingual content (EN to ZH and back) for product teams, DTC brands, and small businesses that need to sound native in both languages, not just "translated into" one.

**What I do:**

- Translate and localize product listings (Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, Tmall) so they read like a local wrote them from scratch
- Write and adapt email campaigns, landing pages, and short ad copy for audiences split across two languages
- Proofread and rewrite machine-translated content before it ships to customers
- Transcribe and translate short-form video (subtitles, captions, voiceover scripts) for creators going cross-market

**How I work:**

You send me the source material, the target audience, and a tone reference (a competitor page, a brand voice doc, or even a "sounds like this" example). I come back with a first draft within 48 hours for pieces under 2,000 words. You give feedback once, I turn the revision in 24 hours. No five-round ping-pong. I work in a shared doc so you see progress in real time and can comment inline.

**Why bilingual matters here:**

Word-for-word translation gets you 70% of the way. The other 30% is knowing that "limited-time offer" carries a different weight in Mandarin than in English, that a product title on Taobao follows different logic than one on Amazon, that a Chinese reader scanning an email subject line expects a different promise structure. I work in both languages daily, so I catch those gaps before your customer does.

**Next step:**

Send me a short sample (even 200 words) and tell me who it's for. I'll return a sample translation or rewrite within 24 hours so you can judge the fit before committing to the full piece.
