## How to use this list

1. **Pick 10 companies** you actually find interesting (you'll write better emails)
2. **Find the founder/CMO/Head of Content** on LinkedIn
3. **Read their last 3 blog posts** so your email references something specific
4. **Send the personalized email** (templates in `emails-templates.md`)
5. **Log every outreach** in `04-tracker/lead-tracker.md`
