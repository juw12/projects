# Cold Email Templates (10 Templates)

Reusable cold email templates for different SaaS company types and contact roles. Replace bracketed sections with personalized details.

---

## Template A: One-off Blog Post

**For jobs like:** *"Blog writer needed for B2B SaaS company"*

```
Hi [Name],

Saw you need a blog post on [topic]. I write SEO content for B2B SaaS
companies — past work in martech, devtools, and productivity software.

A few quick questions before I propose:
- Target keyword + audience (e.g., "decision-maker at mid-market SaaS")
- Tone reference (any posts on your blog you like?)
- Word count + internal links needed

Turnaround for a 1,200-word post: 4 days. One revision included.

I can start this week. Happy to do a short trial post on a different
topic first if you want to test fit before committing.

[Your name]
```

## Template B: Monthly Retainer

**For jobs like:** *"Need a content writer for 4-8 blog posts per month"*

```
Hi [Name],

You need consistent blog output — that's exactly what I do for SaaS
companies. Most of my clients are on monthly retainers (4-8 posts/month)
because it's cheaper than hiring in-house and faster than scaling a team.

Here's how I'd structure the first month:
- Week 1: Keyword research + topic brief for 4 posts (you approve)
- Weeks 2-4: 1 post/week, fully edited, with meta + internal links
- Monthly: 30-min call to review what worked

My rate for retainer work: $[X]/post at 1,200 words. Lower than my
one-off rate because the workflow is smoother.

I'd love to send you a 600-word sample on one of your target topics
before we start — no charge. Lets you test quality without risk.

[Your name]
```

## Template C: Thought Leadership / LinkedIn

**For jobs like:** *"Ghostwriter for SaaS founder LinkedIn presence"*

```
Hi [Name],

I've ghostwritten LinkedIn content for [X] SaaS founders and execs —
the part most people get wrong is sounding like a *real* person, not
a LinkedIn influencer.

My process:
1. 15-min voice note or interview (you talk, I listen)
2. I draft 3 posts/week in your voice (stories, lessons, takes)
3. You approve, I schedule, you stay focused on the business

Average performance for clients: 2-5x impressions vs. their baseline,
with at least 1 post/month hitting 10k+ impressions.

Rate: $[X]/post, or $[Y]/month for 12 posts.

Open to a 2-week trial (3 posts) before signing longer-term.

[Your name]
```

## Template D: Cold Email to SaaS Founder (Comparison Page)

```
Subject: Comparison content idea for [Company]

Hi [First Name],

Saw [Company] just [launched X / raised Y / hit Z milestone] — congrats.

I noticed your blog hasn't posted since [month]. Most SaaS companies
I work with see a 2-3x lift in organic traffic after they start
publishing consistently, and it's the cheapest acquisition channel
to scale.

I write SEO blog posts for B2B SaaS (clients include [types — drop
if none]). I can deliver a 1,200-word post in 4 days, fully researched
and edited.

Would you be open to a trial article on [specific topic relevant to
their product]? If it doesn't perform for you, no hard feelings.

[Your name]
```
