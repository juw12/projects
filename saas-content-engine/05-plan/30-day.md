# Bare Minimum 30-Day Plan (30 min/day)

If everything else is too much, do **only this**. One platform. One service. 30 minutes a day.

## The Setup (Day 1 — 30 minutes total)

**Set up once, then forget:**
- Fiverr account (10 min) — use the SaaS blog gig copy from `01-profiles/fiverr-gigs.md`
- Upload the 3 portfolio pieces (5 min) — from `02-portfolio/`
- Google Sheet tracker with 5 companies (10 min) — headers from `04-tracker/lead-tracker.md`
- Bookmark this plan (5 min)

That's it. Setup is done.

## The Daily 30-Minute Routine

### Every day (5 min)

Open Fiverr → check messages → respond within 4 hours if any.

### Each weekday (Mon-Fri) — pick ONE task, rotate

| Day | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Search Fiverr buyer requests, send 3 proposals | 25 min |
| **Tuesday** | Send 5 cold emails from your target list | 25 min |
| **Wednesday** | Write 1 LinkedIn post for yourself (portfolio building) | 25 min |
| **Thursday** | Search Fiverr buyer requests, send 3 proposals | 25 min |
| **Friday** | Send 5 more cold emails from your target list | 25 min |

### Each weekend (Sat-Sun) — pick ONE task

| Day | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Saturday** | Update tracker: log replies, follow up on warm leads | 20 min |
| **Sunday** | Weekly review (see below) + plan next week | 20 min |

## The Day You Get Your First Client

1. Confirm scope in writing (email recap of what you agreed to)
2. Send a kickoff message ("Got it, here's my plan, deliver by Friday")
3. Use the AI workflow to draft
4. Edit aggressively (your value-add)
5. Deliver 1 day EARLY (always)
6. Ask for a 5-star review immediately after delivery
7. Ask: "Do you have another project coming up?"

---

## The Day Nothing Happens (most days in Week 1-2)

**This is normal.** Most outreach gets no response. Don't:

- Quit
- Spam more
- Change your pitch daily

**Do:**

- Send the next day's outreach anyway
- Look at your 5 most recent "Sent" rows — are they in the right niche?
- Read one good article on cold outreach (Copyblogger, Backlinko) — small adjustments only
