# Daily Action Coach Framework

Use this when you need someone to tell you what to do each day. Replace "me" with your own checklist.

## The Daily Non-Negotiable (15 min/day)

**Do this every single day, even when you don't feel like it:**

1. **Open the tracker** (1 min) — see what's due today
2. **Send 3 outreach messages** (10 min) — Fiverr proposal OR cold email
3. **Reply to any messages** (4 min) — even if just "Got it, will follow up tomorrow"

**That's it.** If you do only this, you'll outlast 90% of people who start.

## The Daily Full Routine (30 min/day, when you have energy)

```
[ ] 5 min   - Check messages on all platforms, respond
[ ] 10 min  - Send 3-5 outreach messages
[ ] 10 min  - Work on one deliverable (draft, edit, polish)
[ ] 5 min   - Update tracker + plan tomorrow
```

## The Weekly Review (Sunday, 20 min)

Answer these 4 questions. Write the answers in your tracker:

1. **How many outreach messages did I send this week?** (Target: 15+)
2. **How many replies?** (Target: 3+)
3. **What's working?** (Best template? Best company type? Best day of week?)
4. **What's not working?** (Lost reasons? Wrong contacts? Bad timing?)

**Then:** Adjust next week's plan based on the answers.

---

## When You're Burned Out

It's normal to hit a wall at Day 5-7, again at Day 14, again at Day 25. When this happens:

**The 5-minute rule:** Just do 5 minutes of the daily routine. Usually you'll keep going. If not, you did your minimum.

**The 1-action rule:** Pick the ONE action that moves you forward most. Just do that. Skip everything else.

**The reset rule:** Take 1-2 full days off. No outreach, no client work. Then come back.

## When Things Go Wrong

### "I got scammed / client won't pay"

- Fiverr/Upwork Resolution Center handles this. Open a dispute. Don't accept work outside the platform.

### "Client is being unreasonable / asking for endless revisions"

- Quote your contract: "X revisions included. Additional revisions at $[Y] each." Most stop after seeing the price.

### "I missed a deadline"

- Message IMMEDIATELY: "Hit an unexpected issue. New delivery: [date]. Sorry for the delay." Most clients forgive honesty.

### "I'm not getting any replies"

- Wait until you have 30+ outreach messages sent. If reply rate is below 5%, change ONE thing (pitch angle, contact target, or subject line). Don't change everything at once.

### "I got a bad review"

- Respond professionally and briefly. Don't argue. Most bad reviews get buried by good ones over time. The goal is 95%+ positive.

## Monthly Milestones (Goal Markers)

| Month | Milestone | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **End of Month 1** | First paying client + 1 review | 🎯 |
| **End of Month 2** | $200-500 earned, 3+ reviews | 🎯 |
| **End of Month 3** | $500-1,000 earned, first retainer client | 🎯 |
| **End of Month 6** | $1,500-3,000/month run rate | 🎯 |
| **End of Month 12** | $3,000-5,000/month, full pipeline | 🎯 |

**If you're behind these targets:** don't quit, but DO diagnose. Common issues:

- Not enough outreach volume (need 30+/week)
- Wrong niche (try a different SaaS vertical)
- Pricing too low (raise by 30%)
- No repeat clients (focus on retainer conversations)
