## Initial brainstorm (2026-11-01)

### Why this project exists

Building a freelance content writing business targeting B2B SaaS companies. Using AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT) as a productivity multiplier. Starting position: no code, no capital, limited time.

### Path chosen

- **Service:** SEO blog posts for B2B SaaS (niche: martech, devtools, AI, productivity)
- **Platform:** Fiverr (primary), Upwork (secondary), cold email (parallel track)
- **First 30 days:** Land 1 paying client

### Open questions to answer

- [ ] Will the SaaS niche generate enough replies vs. broader "blog writer"?
- [ ] Comparison pages (high SEO value) or thought leadership (easier to write)?
- [ ] Cold email or LinkedIn outreach first?
- [ ] $40-60 per post or $100+? Where's the sweet spot?

## Ideas to explore

<!-- Capture ideas here even if they don't get used right away. -->

- **Vertical-specific expertise:** Pick one SaaS vertical (e.g., fintech or devtools) and become "the" writer for that niche. Higher rates, less competition.
- **AI workflow documentation:** Sell the workflow templates I use as a small product on Gumroad ($19-49 each).
- **Newsletter:** "AI content workflows for B2B SaaS" — could become a content marketing channel AND a client source.
- **Comparison page service:** Package as a specific Fiverr gig with fixed scope and pricing.
- **Retainer-first approach:** Skip one-off gigs, only offer monthly retainers (4-8 posts/month). Less volume, higher revenue per client.
